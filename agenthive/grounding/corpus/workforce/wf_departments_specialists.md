## Picking the right specialist

- **"We need to rank for…" / "our website traffic is flat"** → SEO Strategist (audit first, then briefs).
- **"Plan our LinkedIn / Instagram for next month"** → Social Media Planner.
- **"Proposal for a prospect" / "pitch deck"** → Proposal Writer. Give the prices and proof points in the brief.
- **"Write down how we do X" / "handoff keeps breaking"** → SOP Builder.
- **"Launch a product / enter a new segment"** → Go-To-Market Strategist.
- **"Announce something" / "a whitepaper for our buyers"** → Long-Form Writer.
- **Several deliverables** → hire the orchestrator (Agent Workforce): it scopes the job, picks the specialists and delivers each file after QA.

## Deliverable standards (summary)

| Deliverable | Length | Must have |
|---|---|---|
| SEO audit | 1,000-1,600 words + tables | Scorecard, issues with page and fix, prioritised action plan with owners and dates |
| Blog post brief | 500-900 words | Title under 60 characters, meta 120-155 characters, keywords, H2 outline, approved proof, call to action |
| Content calendar | One row per post | Brand cadence, pillar mix within 5 points of target, approved hashtags (max 3) |
| Client proposal | 1,200-2,000 words | Client's own words for the pain, prices exactly as given, approved proof, dated next step |
| Pitch deck | 7-10 slides | One message per slide, at most 6 bullets, last slide a dated next step |
| SOP | 8-20 steps | RACI (one A per row), each step: verb, owner, system, done-when; escalation with time limits |
| Go-to-market plan | 10-14 slides | Sourced market sizing, ICP, positioning, phased launch, KPIs with targets, risks |
| Whitepaper | 1,500-2,500 words | Cited public facts, approved results only, boilerplate verbatim |
| Press release | 400-600 words | Status words match approved facts (beta vs generally available), approved spokesperson, boilerplate, media contact |
