# Pricing — illustrative, not final

Owner: Wursta Agent Workforce product team. Last reviewed 2026-11-01. Prototype documentation for the Wursta Agent Hive.

> **Illustrative pricing, not final.** The figures below are mock values used to demonstrate the estimate step. Final pricing is still to be decided. Always label prices as illustrative when you quote them.

## How a job is priced

Agent Workforce prices each job on **time and materials**: a per-agent rate times the specialist agents assigned to the job. One billable agent is one specialist working on one deliverable. Orchestration, Brand Intelligence, QA & fact-check and document rendering are included.

| Priority tier | Mode | Rate per agent | Example: 3-agent job |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Urgent | Immediate — starts right away | $7.50 | ~$27 |
| P2 Standard | Queued — starts when capacity frees up | $5.00 | ~$18 |
| P3 Economy | Batch — runs in the next batch window | $2.50 | ~$9 |

The example prices include the 20% buffer that is reserved upfront (for example P1: 3 × $7.50 = $22.50 plus a $4.50 buffer = $27.00 reserved).

## Credits

- Customers buy credits in advance: **1 credit = $1**.
- When a job is approved, its estimate plus a 20% buffer is reserved from the credit balance.
- The **unused buffer is refunded** to the balance when the job closes.
- If the balance is too low, the estimate shows the top-up needed before the job can start.

## Revisions

A revision is estimated like a job: the per-agent rate times the agents the change needs — often one.

## Gemini Enterprise customers

In the Gemini Enterprise Agent Marketplace mode, billing runs through the customer's Gemini Enterprise subscription instead of credits.
