# Public Records Ordinance — Staff Guide (PAMC Chapter 2.48)

Records Division, Office of the City Clerk, City of Port Alder. Fictional document for demonstration purposes.

## 1. Right of access

Any person may inspect or obtain copies of public records of the City of Port Alder, for any purpose, without stating
a reason. A public record is any information relating to the conduct of city business that is prepared, owned, used or
retained by the city, in any format — including emails and messages on personal devices when they concern city
business. Requests may be made in writing by email (records@portalder.gov), on the portal, by mail or in person.

## 2. Deadlines (PAMC 2.48.040)

- **Acknowledge** the request within **2 business days** of receipt.
- **Respond** within **10 business days** of receipt: provide the records, an estimate of when they will be
  provided, or a written denial citing the exemption.
- **One extension** of up to **10 additional business days** is allowed when the records are voluminous, held in
  remote storage, or require consultation with another agency or the City Attorney. The extension notice must be sent
  **before the original deadline** and state the reason and the new date.
- **Clarification:** if a request is unclear or too broad to search, the City may ask the requester to clarify. The
  clock **pauses** from the day the clarification is sent and restarts when the requester answers, with the business
  days already used counted. If the requester does not answer within **30 calendar days**, the request is closed.
- Business days exclude weekends and city holidays (2026–27: Harbor Day 12 Oct, Founders' Day 11 Nov, Harvest Day
  26 Nov, Christmas Day 25 Dec, New Year's Day 1 Jan, Constitution Day 15 Feb).

## 3. Fees (PAMC 2.48.060)

The first **2 hours** of search and the first **100 pages** are free. Paper copies cost **$0.10 per page** after
that; electronic delivery is free. Staff time beyond 2 hours is charged at **$28 per hour** only when the request
requires extensive retrieval or redaction. A **deposit** is required when the estimate exceeds **$100**. Fees are
waived for requests in the public interest by media or nonprofits when the records will be published.

## 4. Exemptions (PAMC 2.48.070)

Records are released in full unless an exemption applies. Exemptions are narrow; when only part of a record is
exempt, the rest is released with redactions.

- **E1 Personal privacy:** home addresses, personal phone numbers and personal email addresses of private
  individuals, dates of birth, national ID numbers and bank account numbers. Names of residents who contact the city
  are generally public; their contact details are not. Business contact details of vendors are public.
- **E2 Active law-enforcement investigations:** while the investigation is open. Dispatch logs (time, location,
  call type) remain public.
- **E3 Attorney-client privilege and attorney work product:** communications with the City Attorney seeking or giving
  legal advice. Withheld in full and listed on a privilege log (date, author, recipients, general subject).
- **E4 Deliberative process:** drafts and pre-decisional opinions and recommendations. **Factual material in those
  records must be released.** The exemption ends when the decision is final if disclosure would not chill advice.
- **E5 Proprietary bidder information:** trade secrets and proprietary financial statements submitted with bids.
  **Bid prices, bid tabulations and evaluation scores are public after the award.**
- **E6 Security-sensitive infrastructure:** detailed plans of critical infrastructure (water, signals, IT).
- **E7 Personnel records:** except name, title, salary and dates of employment, which are public.
- **E8 Medical information.**

Records relating to a **pending claim or litigation** are not automatically exempt; they must be reviewed with the
City Attorney before release.

## 5. Redaction practice

1. Redact the minimum necessary — words or lines, not whole pages.
2. Mark each redaction with its exemption code (e.g. "[REDACTED E1]").
3. Keep a **redaction log** for each response: record id, what was redacted, exemption code.
4. A second analyst reviews redactions before release. Automated suggestions are a starting point only.

## 6. The response letter

The response letter states: the request and date received; the records provided (count and pages); records withheld
in full and the exemption; the redactions and exemptions applied; fees; and the requester's **right to appeal** to the
City Manager within **30 days**, and then to the Alder County Superior Court. For an extension or a clarification,
send the notice instead and record the date it was sent.

## 7. Roles

Records analysts coordinate searches with custodians, apply redactions and draft letters. Custodians search their
own mailboxes and files within 3 business days of a search request. The City Attorney reviews privilege, litigation
and law-enforcement questions. The City Clerk signs denials.
