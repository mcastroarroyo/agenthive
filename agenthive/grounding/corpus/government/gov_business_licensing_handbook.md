# Business and Professional Licensing Handbook

Business Licensing Office, Office of the City Clerk, City of Port Alder — 200 Civic Plaza, 1st floor. Fictional document
for demonstration purposes. Port Alder Municipal Code (PAMC) Title 5.

## 1. Who needs a licence

Every business operating in Port Alder needs a **General Business Licence (GBL)** — including home-based and online
businesses with a local address, contractors working on city projects and short-term rental operators. Before applying,
the business must be registered with the national business registry agency and have a National Business Registry
number (BRN) and a tax identification number. Some activities need an additional permit or endorsement on top of the GBL.

## 3. Requirements by licence

**General Business Licence:** BRN and tax ID; zoning clearance for the business address (free, issued at the Permit
Center); a certificate of occupancy for commercial premises or a Home Occupation Permit for home-based businesses;
photo ID of the owner or an officer.

**Food Service Establishment:** active GBL; plan-review approval of the kitchen layout for new or remodelled kitchens;
a Certified Food Protection Manager on every shift; pre-opening health inspection; grease interceptor maintenance
contract; fire inspection of the hood suppression system.

**Mobile Food Vendor:** active GBL; signed commissary agreement with a licensed commissary kitchen; vehicle
registration and commercial auto insurance of at least $500,000; Certified Food Protection Manager certificate; route
and location plan; propane certificate if cooking with gas; vehicle health inspection and fire inspection. Vendors
may not operate within **30 metres of a restaurant entrance** or within **150 metres of a school** during school hours.
Vending inside city parks needs a separate Parks concession permit.

**Short-Term Rental:** the unit must be the operator's **primary residence** (two proofs: national ID address, voter
registration or tax filing); life-safety self-inspection checklist and a first-registration inspection; liability
insurance of at least $1,000,000 covering short-term rental use; a local contact available 24/7 who can respond within
30 minutes; a transient occupancy tax account (12% of the nightly rate). **Maximum 120 unhosted nights per calendar
year.** The registration number must appear on every listing.

**Home Occupation Permit:** owner or landlord consent; the business may use no more than 25% of the home's floor area;
no more than 8 customer visits per day; signage no larger than 0.2 square metres.

**Contractor Registration:** national trade licence; general liability insurance of at least $500,000; a $10,000
contractor bond; workers' compensation if there are employees. Building permits can only be pulled — and inspections
only scheduled — while the registration is active.

**Livery Driver Permit:** national driving licence held 3+ years; background check issued within 60 days; no more than
3 moving violations in 3 years; medical fitness certificate.

**Alcohol Beverage Service Endorsement:** active FSE; national alcohol licence; responsible beverage service training
for all servers within 60 days of hire; a public notice posted for 14 days during which neighbours may object.

## 4. Renewals and late penalties

- Licences are **annual** and expire one year after the start of the current term.
- The **renewal window opens 45 days before expiry**; renewal notices are emailed when the window opens.
- Renewing after expiry adds a **late penalty of 10% of the renewal fee for each month or part of a month** late,
  capped at 50%.
- A licence expired for **more than 90 days is cancelled**. It cannot be renewed: the business files a new
  application and pays the full new-application fee.
- Operating without a valid licence can be cited **$250** per violation.
- Open items on the file (for example an expired insurance certificate) must be fixed before a renewal is approved.

## 5. Changes during the term

Report changes within 30 days: new address (needs a new zoning clearance), change of ownership (new licence), more
employees (fee difference charged at renewal), extra seats (FSE seat fee difference due before the new seats are used).

## 6. How to apply

Apply on the city licensing portal or at the Business Licensing Office, 200 Civic Plaza (Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30). Fees
are paid at submission; applications are processed in the order received and the processing times above start when
the application is complete.
