# Port Alder Municipal Code Digest and Administrative Policy Manual (excerpts)

City of Port Alder — Office of the City Clerk and Human Resources. Fictional document for demonstration purposes. This
digest quotes the most frequently asked rules; the full Port Alder Municipal Code (PAMC) prevails if they differ.

## Part A — Ordinances for residents

**Noise (PAMC 9.36).**
- **9.36.030 Quiet hours:** 22:00–07:00 Monday to Friday and 23:00–08:00 Saturday, Sunday and city holidays. During
  quiet hours, sound from a residence may not exceed 55 dBA at the property line.
- **9.36.040 Power equipment:** leaf blowers, lawn mowers and other powered garden equipment may be used only
  **08:00–19:00 Monday to Friday** and **09:00–18:00 Saturday, Sunday and holidays**. First violation: written warning;
  second within 12 months: $100 citation; third: $250.
- **9.36.050 Construction:** permitted construction work 07:00–19:00 Monday to Saturday; no work on Sundays or holidays
  without a noise variance from Building & Safety.
- **9.36.060 Amplified sound at events:** must end by 22:00 and needs an amplified sound permit.
- Report ongoing noise to the Police non-emergency line (555) 010-0100.

**Solid waste (PAMC 8.12).** Carts may be placed at the curb **after 18:00 the day before** collection and must be
removed by **20:00 on collection day**; leaving carts out is a $50 citation after a warning. Bulky items: two free
pickups per household per year, booked through 311.

**Parking (PAMC 10.20).** No vehicle may remain parked on a public street for more than **72 consecutive hours**.
Vehicles may be tagged and towed 72 hours after the warning tag.

**Short-term rentals (PAMC 5.62).** Only in the operator's primary residence; registration required; **maximum 120
unhosted nights per calendar year**; registration number on every listing; 12% transient occupancy tax. Penalty for
operating unregistered: $500 per day of advertising.

**Trees (PAMC 12.16).** Removing a tree with a trunk diameter of **12 inches or more** at chest height on private
property needs a tree removal permit ($75); one replacement tree is required. Street trees belong to the city —
report problems to 311.

**Sidewalk vending (PAMC 5.48).** Vendors need a sidewalk vending permit, must leave 1.2 m clear for pedestrians and may
not operate within 30 m of a restaurant entrance or 150 m of a school during school hours.

**Yard and garage sales (PAMC 5.80).** Up to four per address per calendar year, each no longer than two consecutive
days, 08:00–18:00; no permit needed.

## Part B — Administrative policies for city staff

**AP-104 Gifts and ethics.** Employees may accept gifts worth **$50 or less** per source per calendar year (cash and
gift cards are never allowed). **No gift of any value** may be accepted from a person or company that is bidding on, or
has submitted a proposal for, an open city solicitation, or that has a matter pending before the employee. Gifts that
cannot be accepted are returned with a standard letter or, for perishables, donated and recorded in the gift register.
All gifts over $25 are recorded in the gift register within 5 working days. Procurement evaluators follow the stricter
conflict rules of the Procurement Manual.

**AP-117 Use of generative AI tools.** Staff may use only **city-approved AI tools** that run in the city's cloud tenant
under its data-protection terms. **Personal data of residents or employees, case notes, and confidential or privileged
information must never be entered into public or personal AI services.** AI output must be reviewed by a person before
it is used in a decision or sent to the public, and AI-assisted content sent to residents must say so. Questions go to
the Information Security Office.

**AP-122 Telework.** Eligible employees may telework up to **2 days a week** with supervisor approval and a signed
telework agreement; city data stays on city devices; employees must be reachable during core hours 09:30–15:30.

**AP-130 Travel and per diem.** Pre-approval is required for travel outside Bayshore Province. Per diem for meals:
$55 per full day within Valdoria, $85 abroad; mileage for personal vehicles $0.58 per km. Receipts are submitted within
15 working days of return.

**AP-141 Records retention.** Emails are retained for 2 years unless they belong to a longer record series (contracts:
7 years after expiry; personnel files: 7 years after separation; permits: permanent). Records relevant to a pending
claim, lawsuit or public-records request are under **litigation or records hold** and must not be deleted.

**AP-150 Social media.** Only designated communications staff may post on behalf of the city. Employees posting
personally must not disclose non-public information or present personal views as the city's position.

**Who owns what.** Ordinance interpretation: City Attorney's Office. Administrative policies: Human Resources (AP-104,
AP-122, AP-130), Information Security Office (AP-117), City Clerk (AP-141), Communications (AP-150).
