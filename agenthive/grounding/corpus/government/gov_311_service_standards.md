# Port Alder 311 Service Standards and SLA Catalogue (2026 edition)

City of Port Alder, Republic of Valdoria — Office of the City Manager, 311 Customer Service Center. Fictional
document for demonstration purposes. Effective 1 January 2026; reviewed annually by the 311 Steering Group.

## 1. Purpose and channels

Port Alder 311 is the single front door for non-emergency city services. Residents can reach 311 by dialling 311
(or (555) 010-0311 from outside the city), through the Port Alder 311 app, the city website, the "Port Alder 311"
messaging channel, or in person at the Civic Plaza service counter. Every report becomes a service request (SR) with a
tracking number in the format SR-YYYY-NNNNNN. Residents may report anonymously; residents who leave a name or
messaging number receive status updates when the request is assigned, put on hold or closed.

**Emergencies are never 311 matters.** Fire, smoke, a smell of gas, injuries, crimes in progress and crashes with
injuries must be referred to the emergency line 911 immediately. Noise complaints go to the Police non-emergency line
(555) 010-0100; power outages go to the electric utility outage line (555) 010-0777.

## 2. Priority levels

- **P1 — Urgent.** A hazard to life, safety or traffic flow. Examples: a deep pothole on an arterial street, a dark or
  flashing traffic signal, a water-main break, a tree blocking a road, hazardous dumping (paint, oil, chemicals,
  needles), offensive or hateful graffiti.
- **P2 — Standard.** A defect that affects service but is not an immediate hazard.
- **P3 — Scheduled.** Work that is planned into a programme or a booked appointment (bulky item pickup, abandoned
  vehicle tagging, sidewalk slab replacement).

**Arterial rule.** Every pothole on a designated arterial street is P1 regardless of size. The designated arterials
are Harbor Street, Alder Boulevard, Bayview Avenue, Quarry Road, Station Street and Kestrel Avenue. Triage staff and
automated intake must apply this rule; downgrading an arterial pothole requires a supervisor note.

SLA compliance is measured on closed requests: a request meets its SLA when it is closed within the target hours.
Requests that are still open after their due time count as breached in backlog reports. The city target is 85%
compliance per category per quarter; Public Works reports compliance by council district (D1–D7) to the City Manager
every quarter.

## 4. Duplicate handling

Before a new request is filed, intake must search for open requests of the same category on the same street within 50
house numbers (roughly one block face) or at the same intersection. When a match exists, the resident is added as a
**follower** of the existing request instead of creating a new one: they receive the same updates and the request's
follower count tells crews how many residents are affected. Requests closed in the last 72 hours at the same location
are shown to the resident; if the problem has returned, the original request is reopened.

## 5. Holds, breaches and honest communication

A request may be put **on hold** only for a documented reason: parts on order, a utility locate, weather, or work
that requires another agency. The hold reason and the next milestone (for example a supplier delivery date) must be
recorded in the request history. When a request breaches its SLA, staff tell the resident plainly that the target was
missed, why, and the next confirmed step. Staff must not promise a completion date that is not in the record.

## 6. Category guidance

- **Potholes:** ask for the nearest house number or cross street, the lane, and approximate width and depth. A
  temporary cold patch closes the hazard; permanent hot-mix repair follows within 30 days.
- **Streetlights:** ask for the pole number on the metal tag. Knocked-down poles are made safe within 24 hours; pole
  replacement depends on supplier lead times.
- **Missed collection:** carts must be at the curb by 06:00 on collection day. A missed collection is recollected
  within 48 hours if the cart was out on time.
- **Bulky items:** two free pickups per household per year, up to four items per pickup; items at the curb by 06:00 on
  the booked day. Mattresses must be wrapped in plastic.
- **Abandoned vehicles:** a vehicle parked on a public street for more than 72 hours may be tagged; it is eligible
  for towing 72 hours after the warning tag.

## 7. Data and privacy

Resident contact details are used only to send status updates and are not shown to crews. Photos sent with a report
may be shown to crews and published in anonymised open-data extracts with faces and plates blurred.
