# Building and Event Permits — Zoning Quick Reference and Fee Schedule

Department of Building & Safety (DBS) and Planning Division, City of Port Alder — Permit Center, 200 Civic Plaza,
2nd floor. Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Fee schedule adopted by Council Resolution 2025-212.

## 1. Zoning districts

| Zone | Name | Max height | Max lot coverage | Setbacks front / side / rear |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-1 | Single-Family Residential | 30 ft | 40% | 20 / 5 / 20 ft |
| R-2 | Two-Family / Townhouse | 35 ft | 50% | 15 / 5 / 15 ft |
| MU-2 | Mixed-Use Corridor | 55 ft | 80% | 0 / 0 / 10 ft |
| C-2 | General Commercial | 45 ft | 70% | 10 / 0 / 10 ft |
| I-1 | Light Industrial | 50 ft | 75% | 15 / 10 / 10 ft |
| OS | Open Space / Parks | 20 ft | 5% | 25 ft all sides |

Restaurants, retail and offices are not allowed in R-1 or R-2. Bars and nightclubs in MU-2 and C-2 need a Conditional
Use Permit decided by the Planning Commission after a public hearing. Special events are allowed in OS, MU-2 and C-2
with a special event permit.

## 2. Accessory dwelling units (ADUs)

One ADU is allowed on any R-1 or R-2 lot with a house. Standards: floor area **up to 800 sq ft**; height **up to
18 ft** for a detached ADU; side and rear setbacks **at least 4 ft**; the ADU counts toward lot coverage. No
owner-occupancy requirement; no extra parking within 800 m of a frequent transit stop. A separate address and utility
shut-offs are required. ADUs under **750 sq ft are exempt from development impact fees**.

## 3. Overlay districts

- **Old Town Historic District (HIST-OT):** any exterior change (façade, windows, signage, demolition) needs a
  Certificate of Appropriateness from the Historic Preservation Commission — about 30 days, fee $220.
- **Coastal Flood Zone AE (FLOOD-AE):** an elevation certificate is required; the lowest floor of new habitable space
  must be at least 1 ft above base flood elevation; flood-resistant materials below that level; review fee $150. Decks
  in FLOOD-AE must use corrosion-resistant fasteners and may not enclose the area below.

## 4. Building permit fees

Permit fee by construction valuation:

| Valuation | Fee |
|---|---|
| up to $2,000 | $85 |
| $2,001 – $25,000 | $85 + $14.50 per $1,000 over $2,000 |
| $25,001 – $100,000 | $418.50 + $10.50 per $1,000 over $25,000 |
| $100,001 – $500,000 | $1,206 + $8.25 per $1,000 over $100,000 |
| over $500,000 | $4,506 + $6.00 per $1,000 over $500,000 |

- **Plan review fee:** 65% of the permit fee when plan review is required.
- **Expedited plan review:** +50% of the plan review fee; roughly halves the review time.
- **Development impact fee:** $3.25 per sq ft of new habitable floor area (ADUs under 750 sq ft exempt).
- **Technology & records surcharge:** 4% of the total.
- **Flat fees:** residential rooftop solar up to 15 kW on the standard plan $275; demolition $350 (asbestos survey
  required); fences over 7 ft (or over 4 ft in a front yard) $95.

**Decks** need plan review only when more than 30 inches above grade or larger than 200 sq ft.

**Review times (first cycle, business days):** ADU 20 (expedited 10), residential addition 15 (7), commercial tenant
improvement 20 (10), deck 5 (2), solar 2, demolition 5. Each correction cycle adds about 10 business days; most
corrections come from missing setbacks on the site plan, accessible-restroom clearances and grease-interceptor sizing.

## 5. Required documents (building)

Site plan with lot lines, existing buildings, the new work and all setbacks; floor plans and elevations; structural
calculations where structural work is involved; energy compliance form EC-1; owner authorisation if the applicant is
not the owner. ADUs add a utility connection letter from the Water Utility. Commercial tenant improvements need
architect-stamped plans, a fire and life-safety plan, an accessibility checklist and health-department approval for
food uses.

## 6. Inspections

Inspections follow the approved sequence and each must pass before the next is booked: ADU and additions — foundation,
framing, rough electrical/plumbing/mechanical (rough MEP), insulation, final; decks — footing, framing, final.
Request by **15:00 the business day before**; windows are 08:00–12:00 or 12:30–16:00. Inspections cannot be booked
when the contractor's city registration has lapsed. A failed inspection needs a re-inspection; a no-show or late
cancellation is charged a $95 re-inspection fee.

## 7. Special event permits

| Attendance | Permit fee | Apply at least | Liability insurance |
|---|---|---|---|
| under 500 | $150 | 30 days before | $1,000,000 |
| 500 – 2,499 | $400 | 60 days before | $1,000,000 |
| 2,500 and over | $950 | 90 days before | $2,000,000 |

Add: street closure **$250 per block per day**; park site fee (Bayfront Park Great Lawn $1,200 per day, capacity
5,000); temporary food permit **$85 per vendor**; temporary alcohol endorsement $150; amplified sound permit $75 —
amplified sound must end by **22:00**. Organisers provide one toilet per 250 attendees (at least 10% accessible), one
licensed security guard per 250 attendees, and a police traffic detail for events of 2,500 or more. The City must be
named as additional insured. Documents: site plan, insurance certificate, security plan, waste plan, traffic control
plan and neighbour notification letter for street closures. The Fire Marshal walks the site before opening.
