# MSME Registration Guide — National Business Registry Agency

National Business Registry Agency (NBRA), Republic of Valdoria — fictional business registry agency for demonstration
purposes. Applies to micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) registering online.

## 1. Choosing an entity type

| | Sole proprietorship (SP) | Simplified Limited Company (SLC) | Limited Liability Company (LLC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owners | exactly 1 natural person | 1 to 50 shareholders (people or companies) | 2 to 50 partners |
| Liability | unlimited — personal assets at risk | limited to contributed capital | limited to contributed capital |
| Minimum capital | none | none | $1,000 paid in |
| Founding document | online form | standard articles (template), e-signed by **all** shareholders | notarised deed |
| Registration fee | $25 | $75 | $150 (+ notary) |
| Agency review | 3 business days | 3 business days | 5 business days |

Most micro and small businesses with partners choose the SLC: no notary, no minimum capital and limited liability.

## 2. Step by step

1. **Verify identity** of every owner through the national identity provider (minutes).
2. **Reserve the business name** — $10, valid **30 days**. The reservation must still be valid when the agency
   approves the registration.
3. **Complete the registration form**: activity code, registered address, owners and, for SLC/LLC, the articles or
   deed. SLC articles must be **e-signed by every shareholder**.
4. **Beneficial ownership declaration** (SLC and LLC): name, national ID and percentage of everyone who owns or
   controls **25% or more**.
5. **Pay the registration fee.**
6. **Agency review** and issue of the registration certificate with the **Business Registry Number (BRN)**.
7. **Tax ID** issued automatically by the Revenue Service, normally one business day after registration.
8. **Employer registration with Social Security** — only when hiring; within **10 days of the first hire**; free.
9. **Municipal business licence** where the business operates (in Port Alder: General Business Licence, plus a Home
   Occupation Permit for home-based businesses, a Food Service Establishment Permit for restaurants, and so on).

## 3. Business names

- Names are compared **ignoring case, punctuation, legal suffixes (SP, SLC, LLC, Co., Inc.), "the", "and"/"&" and
  plurals**. A name that is identical after this normalisation, or confusingly similar to a registered name, is
  refused.
- **Restricted words** need written authorisation from the relevant regulator: Bank, Insurance, Trust Company, Stock
  Exchange, University, National, Republic, Ministry, Government.
- Adding a place or a distinctive word usually resolves a conflict (e.g. "Harbor", "Artisan", "of Port Alder").
- If a reservation lapses, the name is released to the public; it can be reserved again for $10 if nobody else has
  taken it.

## 4. Deadlines and incomplete applications

- Pending actions are shown in the applicant's **action tracker** with the owner of each action (applicant, a
  co-founder, the agency, the Revenue Service) and its due date.
- An application cannot be approved while any **blocking action** is open — typically a missing shareholder signature
  or beneficial ownership declaration.
- If the name reservation expires before the blocking actions are complete, the applicant must re-reserve the name
  and the agency review starts again.
- Incomplete applications are **archived 30 days after the name reservation expires**; fees are not refunded.
- Reminders are sent to the person who owns a pending action 7 days and 2 days before it is due.

## 5. After registration

Keep the BRN, tax ID and certificate together — municipalities and banks ask for all three. Annual obligations: file
the annual return and update the beneficial ownership declaration within **15 days** of any change. Hiring triggers
Social Security registration within 10 days of the first hire; failing to register can be fined and employees lose
coverage. Opening a business bank account requires the certificate and the beneficial ownership declaration.

## 6. Fees summary

Name reservation $10 · SP $25 · SLC $75 · LLC $150 · certified copy of certificate $5 · amendment of articles $20 ·
beneficial ownership update free.
