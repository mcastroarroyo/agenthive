# National Producer Registry and 2026–27 Agricultural Programmes

Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD), Republic of Valdoria — fictional agriculture ministry for
demonstration purposes. Applies to the 2026–27 dry season and the 2027 wet season.

## 1. Registering as a producer

Every farmer who wants ministry support must be in the **National Producer Registry (NPR)**. Registration is free.

1. **Verify identity** through the national identity provider (sign-in on the ministry's messaging channel or at an
   agro-service centre). The name, national ID number (format VID 0000-0000-0) and date of birth must match the
   identity record exactly. A mismatch — for example a missing second surname — blocks registration until it is fixed
   by re-verifying or at the agro-service centre with the national ID card.
2. Receive the **producer number** (format NPR-YYYY-NNNNNN).
3. **Register each plot** you farm (see section 2). Programme eligibility is calculated from registered plots only.

## 2. Registering a plantation or crop plot

Information needed for each plot:

- crop (maize, beans, rice, sorghum, coffee, cacao, avocado, plantain, vegetables, sugarcane) and season;
- area in hectares (0.1 to 50 ha);
- **location**: share a location pin from the plot on the messaging channel, or an agro-technician georeferences it;
- **tenure** and its document:
  - *owner with title* — the property title number (checked automatically against the land registry);
  - *lease or rental* — signed agreement covering at least one full season, with the **landowner's national ID number**;
  - *cooperative* — assignment letter from the cooperative with the plot reference;
  - *usufruct or inheritance in process* — usufruct deed or inheritance-in-process certificate;
  - *informal / family land without title* — a **tenure declaration signed by the producer and two witnesses,
    endorsed by the municipal agriculture officer**;
- coffee plots also need the plant count and approximate plant age.

**Review:** 5 business days. A **field verification visit** is required for plots over 5 ha and for informal tenure;
the review then takes up to 10 business days. Plots whose declared area differs by more than 10% from the georeferenced
area are corrected after the visit.

**Season calendar and registration deadlines**

| Season | Planting | Plot registration closes |
|---|---|---|
| 2026–27 dry | November 2026 – February 2027 | 31 December 2026 |
| 2027 wet | May – June 2027 | 28 February 2027 |

## 3. Programmes

**Smallholder Inputs Package (SIP).** Certified seed for up to 1 ha of staples plus two 45 kg bags of fertiliser per
hectare, up to 3 ha. Eligible: verified identity, **total registered area of 3.0 ha or less**, and at least one
registered staple plot (maize, beans, rice or sorghum). Enrol by **28 February 2027**; delivery in March 2027 at the
municipal agro-service centre.

**Fertiliser Voucher (FVP).** 50% of the price of up to four 45 kg bags per hectare (up to 10 ha) at accredited
agro-dealers. Eligible: total area 10 ha or less; **one voucher per producer per season**. Enrol by 31 December 2026.

**Parametric Crop Insurance (PCI).** Rainfall-index insurance: pays automatically when 30-day rainfall at the nearest
station falls below 60% of the 20-year average, up to $350 per insured hectare; no field loss assessment needed. The
ministry pays **70% of the premium for producers with 5 ha or less** and **30% above 5 ha**. Enrol by **15 December
2026**.

**Coffee Renovation Credit (CRR).** Credit through the national development bank to renovate old or rust-affected
coffee: up to **$2,500 per hectare** for up to 5 ha, at **3% a year over 5 years with 2 years' grace**. The plot must be
**georeferenced**, held under title, lease, usufruct or cooperative assignment (not informal tenure), and have **rust
incidence of 20% or more** or plants **15 years or older**. Enrol by 31 March 2027.

## 5. Messaging channel service rules

The Farmer Desk on the ministry's messaging channel answers in short messages with numbered options. It never asks for
passwords, PINs or bank details. Identity is confirmed by the national identity provider, not by typing ID numbers
in the chat; the landowner's ID for a lease is the only third-party ID requested. Every registration is confirmed back
to the producer with a submission number, and producers can reply "AGENT" to reach a technician during office hours.
