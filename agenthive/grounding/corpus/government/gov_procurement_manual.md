# Procurement Manual — Thresholds, Methods, Evaluation and Integrity

Procurement Division, Finance Department, City of Port Alder. Chief Procurement Officer (CPO): Raymond Cho.
Fictional document for demonstration purposes. PAMC Chapter 3.20; revision 2026-1.

## 1. Thresholds and methods

Thresholds apply to the **total potential value** of the contract, **including all option years and renewals**.
Splitting a requirement to stay below a threshold is prohibited: purchases for the same need within 12 months are
aggregated.

**Goods and services**

| Total value | Method | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| under $10,000 | Micro-purchase: one quote; P-card up to $5,000 | Department |
| $10,000 – $49,999 | Informal quotes: at least **3 written quotes**; lowest responsive quote | Department head |
| $50,000 and over | Formal: **ITB** for clearly specified goods/services (lowest responsive, responsible bidder) or **RFP** for complex services (best value); advertised **at least 14 calendar days** | CPO |

**Construction (public works)**

| Total value | Method |
|---|---|
| under $10,000 | One quote from a registered contractor |
| $10,000 – $99,999 | At least 3 written quotes from registered contractors |
| $100,000 and over | Sealed **ITB**, advertised **at least 21 calendar days**; **5% bid bond enclosed with the sealed bid**; **100% performance and payment bonds** before contract execution |

**Professional services (architecture, engineering, surveying):** $50,000 and over by **qualifications-based
selection (QBS)** — firms are ranked on qualifications only and a fair price is then negotiated with the top-ranked
firm; under $50,000 an informal review of at least 3 firms.

**City Council approval** is required for any contract with a total potential value of **$250,000 or more**.

## 2. Exceptions

- **Sole source:** only one supplier can meet the need. Written justification and CPO approval; at $50,000 or more a
  notice of intent is posted for 5 business days so other suppliers can object.
- **Emergency:** an immediate threat to life, health, property or essential services. The City Manager or CPO may
  authorise the purchase; purchases of $50,000 or more are reported to Council within 30 days.
- **Cooperative contracts:** the City may use a contract competitively awarded by another public agency when the CPO
  determines in writing that it offers best value.

## 3. Evaluation rules

**ITB.** Bids are opened publicly at the stated time. A bid is **responsive** only if it includes every required
form and, for construction, the bid bond **enclosed with the sealed bid at the time of opening**. A missing bid bond is
a **material defect that cannot be waived or cured after opening**; documents sent separately after the deadline are
not accepted. Award goes to the lowest responsive, responsible bidder after applying the local preference.

**Local preference.** For ITBs for goods, services and construction, a business with its principal place of business
in Port Alder receives a **5% evaluation preference, capped at $25,000**. The preference is used for evaluation only:
the contract is awarded at the bid price. It does not apply to RFPs or to nationally funded projects.

**RFP.** The solicitation publishes the criteria and weights. **Price must weigh between 20% and 40%.** Price points
= (lowest price ÷ proposer's price) × price weight. Non-price criteria are scored individually by an evaluation
committee of **at least 3 evaluators** (0–10 scale), averaged, then converted to points. The committee chair checks for
outlier scores: an evaluator whose average for a proposer differs from the others by 1.5 points or more must explain
the difference in writing; unexplained outliers are referred to the CPO.

## 4. Conflicts of interest and integrity

Every evaluator signs a **conflict-of-interest declaration** before receiving proposals. An evaluator **must recuse**
if, with respect to any proposer:

1. they or an immediate family member (spouse or domestic partner, parent, child, sibling, or in-law including a
   **brother- or sister-in-law**) is an owner, officer or employee of the proposer;
2. they were employed by the proposer within the last **24 months**;
3. they have a financial interest in the proposer (5% ownership or $2,000 of income in the last 12 months); or
4. they accepted any gift **worth more than $50** from the proposer in the last 12 months.

Gifts of $50 or less and employment ended more than 24 months ago must be **disclosed** but do not require recusal.
An **undisclosed** conflict is referred to the Ethics Officer. When an evaluator is recused after scoring, the CPO
removes that evaluator's scores and the committee's ranking is recalculated from the remaining evaluators; if fewer
than three remain, a replacement evaluator scores all proposals.

**Cone of silence.** From the date a solicitation is issued until the award, bidders may communicate about it only in
writing with the designated procurement contact. Staff who are contacted must redirect the bidder in writing and
report the contact to the CPO, who records a **written determination** (no action, warning or disqualification) in the
solicitation file.

## 5. Protests

A bidder may protest an award decision in writing to the CPO within **5 business days after the notice of intent to
award** is issued. Late protests are rejected without consideration of the merits. The CPO decides within 10 business
days; the decision is final at the administrative level and may be challenged in court by petition for writ of
mandate.

## 6. Pre-award checklist

Method matches the threshold; advertising period met; bonds received (construction); evaluation committee of at least
three with signed declarations; scores reconciled and outliers explained; conflicts resolved and documented; cone of
silence contacts determined; Council approval scheduled when the total potential value is $250,000 or more.
