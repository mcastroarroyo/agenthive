# FY2027 Community and Small Business Grant Guidelines

Office of Community & Economic Development (OCED), City of Port Alder. Fictional document for demonstration purposes.
Applies to the FY2027 funding round (project periods starting from March 2027).

## 1. Open opportunities

| Id | Programme | Who can apply | Award | Match | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NVG-27 | Neighborhood Vitality Grant | Nonprofits; community groups with a fiscal sponsor | $10,000–$50,000 | 10% cash or in-kind | 12 Dec 2026 |
| SBS-27 | Small Business Storefront Improvement Grant | Small businesses (≤25 employees, revenue ≤$2,000,000) in a commercial corridor | $5,000–$25,000 | 25% cash | 30 Nov 2026 |
| YOF-27 | Youth Opportunity Fund | Nonprofits and schools with ≥$100,000 annual budget and ≥2 years operating | $25,000–$100,000 | none | 20 Nov 2026 |
| GPR-27 | Green Port Resilience Micro-grant | Nonprofits, community groups, small businesses | $2,000–$15,000 | none | 15 Jan 2027 |
| CAF-27 | Cultural Arts Fund | Arts nonprofits | $5,000–$40,000 | 20% | closed 15 Oct 2026 |

**Focus areas.** NVG-27: public space, arts and culture, community safety, placemaking. SBS-27: storefronts, façades,
signage, accessibility, lighting. YOF-27: youth employment, summer jobs, skills training and mentoring for young people
aged 14–24 who live in Port Alder. GPR-27: tree planting, flood resilience, rain gardens, stormwater and energy
efficiency.

**Commercial corridors** for SBS-27: Old Town, Eastgate, Millbrook and Station Quarter.

**Match.** The match is calculated on the grant amount requested (e.g. a $18,000 SBS-27 request needs $4,500 cash).
Match must be documented at application with a bank letter, pledge letter or in-kind valuation.

## 2. Threshold requirements (pass/fail)

Applications that fail a threshold requirement are **not scored** until the issue is fixed (where the deadline allows):

1. Eligible applicant type; community groups must name a fiscal sponsor that is a registered nonprofit.
2. Request within the award range.
3. Minimum budget and operating history where stated (YOF-27).
4. Match documented in full where required.
5. SBS-27 only: an active Port Alder business licence, no delinquent city taxes or fees, and written consent from the
   property owner when the applicant is a tenant.
6. Required attachments: project budget, board list (nonprofits), latest financial statements, and letters of
   support where the narrative relies on partners. A certificate of insurance is required **at award**, not at
   application.

## 3. Scoring

Each rubric criterion is scored **0–5** (0 absent, 1 weak, 3 adequate, 5 exceptional); points = score ÷ 5 × weight.
Every score must cite evidence from the application — a quote, figure or attachment.

- **NVG-27:** Community need and impact 30 · Project design and feasibility 25 · Organisational capacity 20 · Budget and
  cost reasonableness 15 · Equity and resident engagement 10.
- **SBS-27:** Visual and functional improvement 30 · Benefit to the commercial corridor 25 · Project readiness (quotes,
  permits, schedule) 20 · Budget 15 · Local contractors and hiring 10.
- **YOF-27:** Need and target population 25 · Programme design 25 · Measurable outcomes and evaluation 20 ·
  Organisational capacity and track record 15 · Budget and cost per participant 15.
- **GPR-27:** Environmental benefit 35 · Community benefit 25 · Feasibility and maintenance plan 25 · Budget 15.

**Funding bands:** 85–100 Highly recommended · 70–84 Recommended · 60–69 Fund only if money remains after all
recommended applications · below 60 Not recommended.

**Reviewer guidance for YOF-27.** Paid placements score higher than unpaid ones. A cost per participant above
$1,200 should be justified (equipment, certification, wages). Outcomes should include completion, credential and
job-placement targets with a data source; programmes with a comparison group or follow-up at six months score 5 on
outcomes. Strong capacity evidence includes a clean audit and prior city or foundation grants completed on time.

## 4. Review process

1. OCED staff check thresholds and completeness within 5 business days of the deadline and may request missing
   attachments once (5 business days to respond).
2. Two independent reviewers score each application; differences of more than 15 points go to a third reviewer.
3. The review panel ranks applications and recommends awards to the OCED Director. Awards over $50,000 require City
   Council approval.
4. Reviewers sign a conflict-of-interest declaration and recuse themselves from applications involving a family
   member, a current or recent (24 months) employer, or an organisation on whose board they serve.

## 5. After award

Grantees sign an agreement, provide the certificate of insurance and receive up to 40% of the award as an advance.
Quarterly reports are required; the final 20% is paid after the final report. Unspent funds are returned. Storefront
grants are paid as reimbursements against paid contractor invoices; work requiring a building permit must have the
permit before work starts.
