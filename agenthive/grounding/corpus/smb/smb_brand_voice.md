# Brand voice and social media guidelines

Owner: Alicia Moreau (Head of Marketing); day-to-day: Ben Hartley (Content & Social). Last reviewed 2026-10-01. Synthetic demo document — Kitewing Software is fictional.

## Our voice

We sound like a great dispatcher: **calm, clear and specific**. Practical, warm, plain-spoken. We lead with the customer's outcome, not with our features, and we write for people who run vans, crews and service desks — not for software buyers.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Specific ("invoices out in 2 days instead of 9") | Vague ("faster invoicing") |
| Warm and human | Cute or jokey about customers' problems |
| Confident | Boastful or hyped |
| Helpful (a tip the reader can use today) | Salesy in every post |

## Words we avoid

Never use: revolutionary, revolutionize, game-changer, synergy, disrupt / disruptive, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, leverage, unlock, seamless / seamlessly, next-gen, supercharge, 10x, robust, rockstar, ninja.

Prefer plain verbs: help, cut, save, fix, finish, plan, schedule.

## Product names

- **Kitewing FieldOps** — full name on first mention, then "FieldOps".
- **Kitewing Assist** — never "Kitewing AI" or "the bot".
- **Route Optimizer** — two words, capitalised.
- **Insights** — capitalised.

## Claims policy

- Every number about results must come from an approved customer story and **name the customer**, or be clearly labelled **"customer-reported"**.
- Only customers approved for public use may be named: Greystone Facilities Group, Copperline Plumbing & Drain, Summit Ridge Fire Protection and Meadowlark Pest Solutions. Bluefin Elevator Services may only appear anonymised. Harbor & Pine Mechanical may not be mentioned.
- **Prospects are never named**, not even as "talking to".
- Employees are named only with their consent (new-hire announcements: ask first; if someone declines, describe the role without the name).
- No comparisons that name competitors.

## LinkedIn post format

- **Hook** on the first line, under 140 characters — a number, a question or a moment from the field.
- **Length:** 400-1,300 characters (hard limit 3,000).
- Structure: hook → the customer's situation → what changed (with the number) → one practical takeaway → **one call to action** ("Read the story", "Join us at booth 14", "Tell us in the comments").
- **Hashtags:** at most 3, from the approved list: #FieldService, #ServiceTechs, #HVAC, #Plumbing, #FacilitiesManagement, #FireProtection, #PestControl, #SmallBusiness, #Kitewing, #AIatWork, #Hiring.
- **Emojis:** at most 2, never in the hook. **Exclamation marks:** at most 1.
- Images: real customer photos only with written permission; otherwise product screenshots or team photos.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Target share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Customer outcomes | 40% | Case-study numbers, quotes, field stories |
| Product & how-to | 25% | New features (Assist in Spanish, Route Optimizer re-sequencing), tips |
| People & culture | 20% | New hires (with consent), awards, team moments |
| Industry insight | 15% | Benchmarks, seasonal planning, technician shortage |

## Cadence

- Company page: **3 posts per week — Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday at 08:30.**
- One employee-advocacy post per week (team members reshare with a personal line).
- Event posts go out the week before and the morning of the event.

## Approval

- Ben Hartley drafts; Alicia Moreau approves customer stories and anything with numbers.
- Customer-story posts also need the customer's written approval of the exact wording when it includes a quote.
- Posts drafted with AI tools follow the AI use policy: a person reviews every post before it is scheduled.

## Examples

**Good hook:** "86% of jobs fixed on the first visit — across five branches."
**Weak hook:** "We're excited to share some amazing news!!!"

**Good claim:** "Greystone Facilities Group now sends invoices in 2 days instead of 9."
**Not allowed:** "Our customers cut dispatch time by 40%." (unnamed, unapproved number)
