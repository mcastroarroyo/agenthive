## CS-01 — Greystone Facilities Group (Enterprise, 210 technicians)

**Challenge:** five branches on three different scheduling tools and SLA penalties on two large property-management contracts.

**Solution:** Enterprise rollout in 11 weeks with multi-branch permissions, single sign-on and Insights dashboards per branch; Route Optimizer added in year two. A Kitewing Assist pilot with 25 technicians started in October 2026.

**Results**
- First-time-fix rate from **71% to 86%**.
- Technician overtime down **22%**.
- Job-to-invoice cycle from **9 days to 2 days**.

**Quote:** "We finally run five branches as one company." — Walter Ashby, VP Field Operations.

## CS-02 — Copperline Plumbing & Drain (Growth, 60 technicians)

**Challenge:** technicians crossed the city twice a day; fuel and overtime were rising.

**Solution:** Route Optimizer with live re-sequencing and drive-time dashboards in Insights.

**Results**
- Drive time per job down **18%**.
- Jobs per technician per day from **4.1 to 4.8**.
- Annual fuel spend down **$46,000**.

**Quote:** "Same team, almost one extra job per tech every day." — Leila Haddon, Service Manager.

## CS-03 — Summit Ridge Fire Protection (Growth, 72 technicians)

**Challenge:** statutory inspections tracked in spreadsheets, reports reaching customers days later, slow payments.

**Solution:** inspection templates on mobile, same-day digital reports, Route Optimizer, and Kitewing Assist for code look-ups and troubleshooting.

**Results**
- Missed inspections down **90%**.
- Days sales outstanding from **52 to 34 days**.
- Technician calls to the office down **35%** with Assist.

**Quote:** "Reports go out before the truck leaves the car park." — Beatriz Lomax, Compliance & Inspections Lead.

## CS-04 — A regional elevator maintenance company (Growth, 48 technicians)

**Challenge:** maintenance contracts renewed late or lost; inspection dates kept in a spreadsheet.

**Solution:** maintenance agreements with automatic renewal reminders and inspection scheduling.

**Result:** maintenance-contract renewal rate from **82% to 93%**.

Reference calls are possible on request through the account owner; never name the customer in writing.

## CS-05 — Meadowlark Pest Solutions (Starter, 24 technicians)
