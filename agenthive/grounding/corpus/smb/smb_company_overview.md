# Kitewing Software — company overview

Kitewing Software, Inc. is a fictional 62-person B2B software company used throughout the Wursta Agent Hive SMB demos. Every company, person and figure in this knowledge base is synthetic.

## What we do

Kitewing builds **Kitewing FieldOps**, a cloud application that helps service contractors run their field operations: scheduling and dispatch, a mobile app for technicians (with offline mode), work orders with photos and signatures, maintenance agreements with recurring billing, parts and inventory, invoicing and a customer portal. Three add-ons extend the core product:

- **Route Optimizer** — daily route optimisation and live re-sequencing when traffic or a cancellation changes the day.
- **Kitewing Assist** — an AI copilot built on Google's Gemini models that answers technicians' troubleshooting questions from manuals and job history, writes job summaries and turns voice notes into work-order text. Spanish-language answers are in beta from 2026-11-17.
- **Insights** — an analytics workspace on BigQuery with prebuilt dashboards and data export (included in Enterprise).

FieldOps runs on Google Cloud in US regions. Kitewing holds a SOC 2 Type II report.

## Who we serve (ideal customer profile)

Service contractors with **10 to 300 field technicians**, one to five branches and recurring maintenance work:

| Segment | Typical customers | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC, plumbing & home services | Residential and light-commercial contractors | Peak-season overtime, first-time fix, maintenance agreements |
| Facilities management | Multi-site maintenance providers | SLAs, multi-branch control, compliance evidence |
| Fire protection, alarm & security | Inspection and installation companies | Statutory inspections, report turnaround, cash flow |
| Elevator maintenance | Regional maintenance firms | Contract renewals, inspection deadlines |
| Pest control & landscaping | Route-based services | Dense routes, seasonal crews, weather rescheduling |

Kitewing serves about 170 active customers. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) is reported in the BigQuery warehouse (table smb_customers).

## Values

1. **Be the calm dispatcher** — clear, specific and kind under pressure.
2. **Customers' outcomes first** — we measure ourselves by their first-time fix, overtime and cash flow.
3. **Small company, high ownership** — one accountable owner for every task.
4. **Learn every week** — four learning hours per week and a $1,500 annual learning budget for everyone.
5. **Safe by default** — security keys, least privilege and no customer data in unapproved tools.
