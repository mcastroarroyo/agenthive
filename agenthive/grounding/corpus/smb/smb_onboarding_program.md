# New-hire onboarding programme and checklist

Owner: Erin Walsh (People & Culture) with Noah Kim (IT & Security). Last reviewed 2026-10-01. Synthetic demo document — Kitewing Software is fictional.

Our goal: every new hire has a working laptop, every account they need and a clear 30/60/90 plan **on day one**, and meets their day-30 goals.

## Pre-boarding timeline

| When (business days before start) | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| T-10 | Offer signed; employee record created in the HR portal | Erin Walsh |
| T-7 | Provisioning tickets opened for equipment and accounts (role template) | Hiring manager with Noah Kim |
| T-5 | People welcome email: HR portal invite, benefits pack, handbook link | Erin Walsh |
| T-3 | Manager welcome email with the day-one agenda and first-week goals | Hiring manager |
| T-2 | Buddy books the day-one lunch and a 15-minute daily check-in for week one | Buddy |
| T-1 | Laptop delivered (office desk or home), security keys ready, accounts created | Noah Kim |

Laptops take up to **5 business days** to configure and ship. If a ticket is opened late, IT marks it "tight" or "at risk" — expedite immediately.

## Day one

Office day one starts at 08:45 at reception (Cedar Harbor HQ) or with a welcome video call for remote hires. The standard agenda:

1. Welcome coffee with your manager.
2. IT setup with Noah Kim: laptop, Google Workspace, MFA with security keys, password manager.
3. People & Culture session with Erin Walsh: handbook, benefits enrolment, HR portal.
4. Kitewing story, mission and customers (self-paced video).
5. Team lunch with your buddy (remote hires: virtual lunch with a $25 meal stipend).
6. Role kickoff: your 30/60/90 plan with your manager.
7. Product tour of the Kitewing FieldOps demo tenant (Sales: Rosa Delgado; Product & Engineering: Sofia Petrov; Customer Success: Ines Duarte).
8. Security awareness training (required within 7 days — most people start it on day one).
9. A role session (for example the CRM and pipeline walkthrough for account executives, dev-environment setup for engineers, shadowing a customer call for implementation consultants).
10. Day-one wrap-up with your buddy.

**Hybrid hires** whose start date falls on a Monday or Friday have a remote day one; their first office day is the next Tuesday, starting with badge and desk set-up with Erin Walsh at 09:00.

## The 30/60/90 plan

- Agreed with your manager in the day-one role kickoff, based on your role template plus your first assignment.
- **Day 30:** learning and first contributions (certifications done, shadowing, first small deliverable). Check-in with your manager and Erin Walsh.
- **Day 60:** owning work with support (first solo calls, first feature, co-led implementation).
- **Day 90:** full ownership and the end of probation; review with your manager.
- Goals always have a measure ("pipeline of $250k with MEDDICC ≥ 60%", "PR merged by day 5").

## Buddy programme

- Every new hire gets a buddy from their own team with at least one year at Kitewing.
- Week one: 15-minute daily check-in. Days 8-30: weekly coffee. The buddy is your first stop for "how do we do X here?"
- Buddy hours count as mentoring in the career framework.

## Access rules

- Accounts follow least privilege and the role template; extra access needs a ticket and your manager's approval.
- **Google Cloud console (development project)** access is granted through a group with manager approval.
- **BigQuery read access** to the development dataset needs approval from the data owner (Femi Adeyemi).
- **On-call paging** is added on day 30, after the incident-response training. Production access is never granted in the first 90 days.
- Implementation consultants receive the data-migration SFTP only after manager approval.
