# Career framework, skills matrix and learning policy

Owner: Erin Walsh (People & Culture) with team leads. Last reviewed 2026-09-10. Synthetic demo document — Kitewing Software is fictional.

## Skill levels

Every skill in the framework is assessed on four levels. Evidence matters more than years of experience.

| Level | Name | What it means | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aware | Understands the concepts; needs guidance to apply them | Course completed, shadowing |
| 2 | Working | Applies the skill independently on routine work | Delivered tasks without help |
| 3 | Practitioner | Handles complex cases and helps others | Led a project or workstream; reviews others' work |
| 4 | Expert | Sets the standard; teaches and improves how Kitewing works | Wrote the playbook, trains others, recognised externally |

Readiness for a role is the share of required levels a person already meets. 80% or more with no gap larger than one level is the usual bar for promotion.

## Career levels

- **Individual contributor:** P1 (Associate), P2 (Professional), P3 (Senior), P4 (Staff / Principal).
- **Management:** M1 (Team Lead), M2 (Manager), M3 (Head of / Director).
- Moving between tracks is a lateral move, not a promotion.

## Promotion process

1. Promotions are decided twice a year, in **March and September**, together with performance reviews.
2. The employee and manager prepare a short case: the target role, readiness against the role matrix and three pieces of evidence (projects, feedback, results).
3. A calibration panel (team lead, Erin Walsh and one leader from another team) reviews every case for consistency.
4. Decisions and feedback are shared within two weeks.

## Learning policy

- **Budget:** $1,500 per employee per year for courses, books and conferences, approved by your manager.
- **Time:** four learning hours per week, usually on Friday afternoons ("focus Fridays").
- **Google Cloud certifications:** exam fees (for example Cloud Digital Leader, Associate Cloud Engineer, Professional Cloud Architect, Professional Data Engineer, Professional Cloud Developer, Generative AI Leader) are **reimbursed on pass** and do not count against the annual budget. Take associate-level exams before professional-level exams.
- **Google Cloud Skills Boost:** company-paid subscription for every employee.
- **Kitewing Academy:** internal courses — Kitewing Certified Associate and Certified Expert, Kitewing Delivery Method, Data migration toolkit, Train-the-trainer, MEDDICC & discovery bootcamp, Demo certification, Solution design clinic, Secure coding.

## Stretch assignments

The fastest way to grow a skill is real work with a safety net. Examples:

| Skill | Stretch assignment |
|---|---|
| Solution design | Co-design the solution for a new Enterprise deal with the sales engineer |
| Project management | Run the weekly status for a customer implementation |
| Data governance | Own the Insights data-quality scorecard for a quarter |
| Mentoring | Be a buddy for a new hire |
| Executive communication | Present the QBR for one of your accounts to the customer's executive |
| System design | Shadow six architecture reviews, then write one design doc |

## Development plans

- Every employee has a development plan reviewed with their manager each quarter: target role, top three gaps, learning path with dates and one stretch assignment.
- Learning paths go **course first, then on-the-job practice, then certification**.
- Track progress in the learning portal; certifications are added to your profile in the HR portal.

## Hiring and skills

- Candidates are assessed against the same role matrix, using evidence from their CV and structured interviews.
- Interviews probe the largest gaps with behavioural questions ("Tell me about a time you…").
- Assessments consider skills and evidence only — never age, gender, health, family status or any other protected characteristic.
