# Kitewing employee handbook (2026 edition)

Owner: Erin Walsh, People & Culture Lead. Last reviewed 2026-09-01. Synthetic demo document — Kitewing Software is fictional.

## Working hours and where we work

- **Core hours** are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone; outside core hours, plan your day with your team.
- **Office-based and hybrid** employees work in the office Tuesday to Thursday (Cedar Harbor HQ or Millbrook). Monday and Friday are remote days for hybrid employees.
- **Remote** employees visit headquarters once per quarter for a planning week; Kitewing pays travel and accommodation.
- **Focus Fridays:** Friday afternoons are meeting-free. Use them for deep work and your four weekly learning hours.

## Time off

| Type | Allowance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vacation | 20 days per year | Accrues at 1.67 days per month from your start date. In your first 3 months you may borrow up to 5 days with your manager's approval. |
| Company holidays | 10 days per year | Published in the HR portal each December, including the year-end shutdown. |
| Floating holidays | 2 days per year | Use them for any personal or cultural occasion. |
| Sick leave | 8 days per year | No doctor's note needed for absences of up to 3 consecutive days. |
| Parental leave | 16 weeks paid (primary caregiver), 6 weeks paid (secondary caregiver) | Available from day one. |
| Bereavement | Up to 5 days | Speak to your manager or Erin. |

- **Year-end shutdown:** the company closes from 24 December to 1 January. Support runs a skeleton rota with time off in lieu.
- **Carry-over:** up to 5 unused vacation days carry into the next year and must be used by 31 March.
- Request time off in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for anything longer than 3 days. Customer-facing teams also check the team leave calendar so two people in the same role are not away at once.

## Pay and benefits

- **Payroll** runs twice a month: on the 15th and on the last business day. Questions go to Maya Singh (Finance Manager).
- **Health, dental and vision:** Kitewing pays 90% of the premium for employees and 70% for dependants. Enrol within **30 days of your start date** in the HR portal; after that you must wait for open enrolment in November.
- **Retirement plan** with a 4% employer match, vesting immediately.
- **Home office:** a one-time $500 stipend for remote and hybrid employees plus $40 per month towards internet.
- **Phone stipend:** $50 per month for customer-facing roles (sales, customer success, support), approved by your manager.
- **Learning:** $1,500 per person per year plus four learning hours per week. Google Cloud certification exam fees are reimbursed on pass and do not count against the budget. See the career framework.

## Expenses and travel

- Submit expenses in the expense tool **within 30 days**, with a receipt for anything over $25.
- Travel meals: up to $60 per day. Book travel in economy through the travel tool at least 14 days ahead where possible.
- Customer entertainment above $150 needs your manager's approval in advance.
- On a remote day one, new hires may expense a $25 lunch for the virtual team lunch.

## Equipment and security

- Everyone receives a company laptop (MacBook Pro 14-inch as standard; engineers get a 16-inch model with 32 GB), a 27-inch monitor, a headset and **two hardware security keys**. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
- Multi-factor authentication with a security key is mandatory for Google Workspace and every system that supports it. Store passwords only in the company password manager.
- Report a lost or stolen device to Noah Kim (IT & Security) **within 1 hour**.
- Never put customer data into personal tools or unapproved AI tools. AI use follows the AI adoption guide and use policy.
- Complete security awareness training within 7 days of joining and every year after.

## Conduct

- We follow the code of conduct and respectful workplace policy (training within 14 days of joining).
- Report harassment, discrimination or safety concerns to your manager, to Erin Walsh, or through the anonymous reporting line linked in the HR portal. Retaliation is never tolerated.
- Declare conflicts of interest (side businesses, family members at customers or vendors) to Erin Walsh.
- Gifts from customers or vendors worth more than $75 must be declared.

## Performance and growth

- New hires work to a **30/60/90-day plan** agreed with their manager in week one. The first 90 days are a probation period with check-ins at days 30, 60 and 90.
- Performance and growth reviews run twice a year, in March and September. Promotions are decided in the same cycles (see the career framework).
- Weekly 1:1s with your manager are the default; your buddy checks in daily in week one.
