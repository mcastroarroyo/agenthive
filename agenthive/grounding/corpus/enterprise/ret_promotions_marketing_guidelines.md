# Larkspur Promotions and Marketing Guidelines

These guidelines govern every promotion and marketing campaign at Larkspur Home & Tech: how deep discounts can go, when promotions are blocked, how often customers can be contacted, and what campaign copy must say. The Promotions and Inventory Campaign agents check plans against them.

Fictional guidelines for the Wursta Agent Hive demo. Larkspur Home & Tech and its customer segments are invented.

## Margin and discount rules

- **Margin floor:** gross margin at the promotional price must be at least **18%** of the promotional price.
- **End-of-life clearance exception:** the floor drops to **0%** (no selling below cost) for end-of-life items, with merchandising director sign-off.
- **No stacking:** one promotional mechanic per item; coupons do not stack with promotional prices.
- Any discount above the category maximum needs **merchandising director** approval.

| Category | Maximum discount |
|---|---|
| TV & Home Theater | 25% |
| Computing | 20% |
| Audio | 35% |
| Smart Home | 35% |
| Kitchen | 30% |
| Gaming | 30% (consoles 10%) |
| Networking | 30% |
| Wearables | 40% |

## Blackout periods

| Period | Dates | Categories | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black Friday quiet period | 2026-11-23 to 2026-11-30 | TV & Home Theater, Computing, Gaming | No new promotions in doorbuster categories in the week before and during Black Friday |
| Price-lock week | 2026-12-19 to 2026-12-26 | All categories | No new promotions; holiday price-match guarantee in force |

Campaigns for TVs, computing or gaming in November must therefore run and end before 2026-11-23.

## Offer mechanics

- **Public price promotion:** the shelf and online price drops for everyone. It lifts all sales but gives the discount away on units that would have sold at full price anyway.
- **Targeted offer code:** a personal code sent only to the chosen segments. Baseline sales stay at full price, so it is the **preferred mechanic for overstock and end-of-life campaigns**. Codes are single-use and expire with the campaign.
- **Bundle:** a discount on an attach item (for example the soundbar with a TV). Counts as the item's one mechanic.
- Small-ticket items (under $150) should share one multi-SKU campaign, such as "gifts under $100", so the fixed campaign cost of about $1,500 is paid once.

## Contact frequency caps

- **Email:** at most **2** promotional emails per segment per week.
- **Push:** at most **3** per week.
- **SMS:** at most **1** per week.

When a segment is at its cap, start the campaign the following week or use another channel with capacity.

## Customer segments

| Id | Segment | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| SEG-HCU | Home Cinema Upgraders | Bought a TV 3+ years ago or browsed large TVs in the last 60 days |
| SEG-DLR | Deal Seekers | Respond mostly to deep discounts; high redemption, low full-price follow-on |
| SEG-GFT | Holiday Gifters | Q4 gift buyers; respond to "gifts under $100" framing |
| SEG-NWH | New Homeowners | Moved in the last 6 months |
| SEG-STU | Students & First Apartments | Student verification or age-gated app sign-up |
| SEG-GMR | Core Gamers | Bought gaming hardware in the last 18 months |
| SEG-FIT | Wellness & Fitness | Bought wearables or blenders |
| SEG-LOY | Larkspur Rewards Gold | Top-tier loyalty members |

**Rewards Gold is margin-protected:** no straight discounts above 10%. Use bonus points, bundles or early access instead.

## Required copy elements

- **"While stocks last"** on every overstock or clearance offer (email and social).
- **Offer end date** in every email ("Ends Nov 22").
- **SMS** must include "Reply STOP to opt out".
- The discount stated in copy must match the offer; a bundle discount (for example 20% off the soundbar) may be stated for the bundled item.
- Brand names: **Larkspur** and **Larkspur Rewards**, never "LarkSpur" or all caps.

## Banned phrases

cheapest, lowest price ever, guaranteed, best price anywhere, "!!!", act now.

## Channel limits

| Channel | Limit |
|---|---|
| Email subject | 60 characters |
| Push | 110 characters |
| SMS | 160 characters |
| Social | 280 characters |

## Campaign brief template

Every campaign brief contains:

1. **Objective:** clear overstock, acquisition or loyalty, with the target (for example "bring LUM-55Q8 below 60 days of cover").
2. **Offer:** SKUs, discount or bundle, start and end dates.
3. **Audience:** segment ids, channel, estimated sends and expected buyers.
4. **Economics:** promo margin %, expected units and net margin, margin versus doing nothing, campaign cost.
5. **Guardrail checks:** category maximum, margin floor, calendar and blackout conflicts, contact caps.
6. **Copy:** email subject and body, push, social, SMS if used.
7. **Approvals needed** and the measurement plan (holdout or matched stores).

## Approvals

- Within all rules: marketing manager approves and schedules.
- Above category maximum or below margin floor: merchandising director.
- Any campaign during a blackout: not allowed.
