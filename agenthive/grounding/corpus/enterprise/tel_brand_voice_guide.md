# Kestrava Brand Voice and Copy Rules

This guide tells writers, agencies and the Content Creator agent how Kestrava Telecom sounds and which rules every piece of consumer marketing copy must pass before it ships. The brand-compliance critic checks exactly these rules.

Fictional brand guide for the Wursta Agent Hive demo. Kestrava Telecom is an invented company.

## Voice principles

- **Clear before clever.** If a pun hides the benefit, cut the pun.
- **Warm, confident, never pushy.** No countdown pressure, no shouting.
- **Talk to one person.** Use "you" and "your" more than "we", "our" or "us".
- **Short sentences.** Average 20 words or fewer per sentence.
- **Plain words.** No network jargon in consumer copy (say "faster evenings", not "lower contention ratio").

## Product names

Always write product names exactly like this:

| Correct | Never |
|---|---|
| Kestrava | KesTrava, KESTRAVA |
| Kestrava Fiber Max | Fibre Max, Fiber-Max, FiberMax |
| Kestrava 5G Home | 5G home, Kestrava 5g |
| Kestrava One | Kestrava ONE |
| Kestrava Wi-Fi 7 Gateway | (no variants) |

## Banned terms

These words are never used: revolutionary, game-changer, game changer, best-in-class, cheap, guaranteed, no-brainer, insane, blazing, act now, "limited time only!!".

Replace them with a concrete, provable benefit ("2 Gig for every device at once", "install in one visit").

## Claims that need a source

The following claims are only allowed with an asterisk on the claim and a "Source:" line in the same piece: fastest, #1, number one, best network, most reliable, lowest price.

Example: "Rated fastest home internet in Port Calder*" followed by "*Source: Fictional Speed Index Q3 2026."

## Required legal lines

| When the copy... | It must include |
|---|---|
| says "unlimited" | Fair use policy applies. |
| shows a price in push, SMS or social | T&Cs apply. |
| shows a price in email | Taxes and fees extra. T&Cs apply. |
| is an SMS | Reply STOP to opt out |

## Channel rules

| Channel | Length | Emoji | Hashtags | Exclamation marks | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Push | Body 110 characters, title 40 characters | max 1 | none | max 1 | required |
| SMS | 160 characters including "Reply STOP to opt out" | 0 | none | max 1 | required |
| Email | Subject 50 characters, preheader 90 characters, body 180 words | max 1 | none | max 1 | required |
| Social | 280 characters | max 2 | max 3 | max 1 | required |

Email copy is submitted with labelled lines so it can be checked: `Subject:`, `Preheader:`, `Body:` and `CTA:`. Push copy may start with a `Title:` line.

## Calls to action

Start the call to action with one of these verbs: Upgrade, Get, Switch, Check, Find, Join, Try, See, Book, Claim. Keep it to five words or fewer ("Check your address", "Upgrade to 2 Gig").

## Case and capitals

- Use sentence case for headlines and subjects.
- No ALL-CAPS words of four letters or more, except the allowed terms: 5G, TV, HD, 4K, SMS, NBA, T&Cs, STOP, MLO, Wi-Fi, Gig.

## Scoring

The critic scores each piece out of 100: minus 25 per blocker, minus 10 per major issue, minus 3 per minor issue. A piece **passes** only with no blocker and no major issue.

- **Blockers:** banned terms, unsourced claims, missing fair-use line, SMS without opt-out or over 160 characters.
- **Major:** length limits, emoji limits, product-name spelling, missing price legal line, missing call to action.
- **Minor:** exclamation marks, ALL-CAPS, long sentences, "we" outnumbering "you", too many hashtags.

## Good and bad examples

Bad push (fails: banned term, two emoji, shouting, no legal line):

> Revolutionary 2 Gig is HERE!! 🚀🔥 Only $65/mo. Act now!

Good push (passes):

> Title: Your evenings, faster
> Everyone online at once, no waiting. Upgrade to Kestrava Fiber Max 2 Gig for $65/mo. T&Cs apply.

Good email:

> Subject: Kestrava Fiber Max 2 Gig is here
> Preheader: Stream, game and work at once without the wait.
> Body: Your home has more screens than ever. Fiber Max 2 Gig gives every one of them room to breathe. Get 2 Gig for $65/mo for 12 months. Taxes and fees extra. T&Cs apply. Offer ends 31 Dec 2026.
> CTA: Upgrade to 2 Gig

Good SMS:

> Kestrava: 2 Gig fiber for $65/mo for 12 months. Check your address at kestrava.example/2gig. T&Cs apply. Reply STOP to opt out
