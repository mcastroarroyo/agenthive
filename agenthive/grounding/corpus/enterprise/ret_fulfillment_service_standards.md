# Larkspur Fulfillment Service Standards

These standards define the delivery promises Larkspur Home & Tech makes to online customers, how carriers are chosen, and how the team handles late, split, damaged or undeliverable orders. The Fulfillment agent and the support desk apply them.

Fictional standards for the Wursta Agent Hive demo. Larkspur Home & Tech and all carriers named are invented.

## Delivery promises

| Service | Promise | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | All regions |
| Express | 1-2 days | All regions |
| Next-day and same-day | Next day, or same day for orders before noon | Coast and Central (Coastal Courier) |
| White-glove | 3-5 days with a booked delivery slot | All regions, large TVs |
| Store pickup | Ready the same or next day | Any store with stock |

Online orders ship from Ashford Vale DC or Redmarsh DC (operated by Halvard Logistics), or from a store with stock (ship-from-store).

## Carriers

## Carrier selection rule

1. Consider only carriers with **92% or better on-time** delivery over the last 30 days.
2. Choose the **cheapest** option that meets the promised date.
3. If no reliable option meets the promise, choose the **fastest reliable** option and notify the customer.
4. Active **weather advisories** add days to the estimate for affected regions (for example 2 extra days in the North during the ice storm closing Northgate Pass).

## Split shipments and ship-from-store

- Ship what is available; a split order must not delay the in-stock items.
- Ship-from-store is preferred for backorders when a store in the same region has stock.
- A store pick missed twice is escalated to the store manager; if it is not picked by 14:00, reallocate to another store.

## Exceptions and what to do

| Exception | Action |
|---|---|
| Backorder | Offer ship-from-store, a new date or cancellation; let the customer choose |
| Carrier delay | Proactive delay notice with the new date; no reship while in transit |
| Split shipment | Escalate the store pick; reallocate if needed |
| Address issue | Contact the customer within 2 hours; the express clock pauses until confirmed |
| Failed delivery | After 2 attempts the parcel is held at the depot for 5 days; offer a new slot or store pickup |
| Damaged in transit | If reported within 48 hours, ship a replacement now and open a carrier claim |
| DC backlog | Check carrier options to protect express promises; notify if they cannot be met |

## Notification standard

- Notify the customer within **2 hours** of detecting an exception, before they ask.
- Every message includes:
  1. The **order id**.
  2. **What happened**, in one plain sentence (no internal codes).
  3. The **new delivery date**, or the choice the customer needs to make.
  4. **What we are doing** about it.
  5. The **credit**, if one applies.
- Email is the default; SMS for same-day changes, maximum two segments (320 characters).

## Compensation

| Situation | Goodwill credit |
|---|---|
| Delivered 2 or more days late | $10 credit |
| 5 or more days late, or an express promise missed | $20 credit plus a shipping refund |
| Damaged in transit | Free replacement; no credit unless also late |

- Credits up to **$25** are auto-approved.
- Larger credits need **supervisor approval**; hold the message until approved and tell the customer when they will hear back.

## Service metrics

The fulfilment team reviews weekly: on-time delivery % by carrier and service, exceptions by type, median time from detection to customer notice (target under 2 hours), credits issued, and repeat exceptions for the same customer.
