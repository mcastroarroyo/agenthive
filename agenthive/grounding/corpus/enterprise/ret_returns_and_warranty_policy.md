# Larkspur Returns, Exchanges and Warranty Policy

This policy tells store teams, the customer-support desk and the Support Scout agent when Larkspur Home & Tech accepts a return or exchange, what fees apply, and when a case moves to warranty or escalation.

Fictional policy for the Wursta Agent Hive demo. Larkspur Home & Tech is an invented retailer.

## Return windows

| Item | Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Most items | 30 days | Opened or unopened |
| TVs 55 inches and larger, opened | 15 days | Unopened: 30 days |
| Laptops, opened | 15 days | Unopened: 30 days |
| Game consoles, opened | 15 days | Unopened: 30 days |

- The window starts on the **delivery date** for shipped orders, or the purchase date in store.
- **Larkspur Rewards Gold** members get **15 extra days** on every window.
- **Holiday returns:** items bought from 2026-11-01 to 2026-12-24 can be returned until **2027-01-31**, if that is later than the normal window.

## Restocking fees

| Item (opened, returned for change of mind) | Fee |
|---|---|
| Laptops | 15% |
| TVs 55 inches and larger | 10% |
| Game consoles | 10% |
| Everything else | none |

Fees are **waived** when the reason is defective, damaged in transit, wrong item or not as described, and always waived for Rewards Gold members.

## Defective and damaged items

- **Dead on arrival:** a defect reported within **14 days** of delivery gets an exchange or refund with no fee.
- **Defective within the return window:** exchange preferred, refund allowed, no restocking fee.
- **Defective after the window:** the case goes to the manufacturer warranty (12 months by default; Lumetra TVs 24 months) or to Larkspur CarePlus if the customer bought it.
- **Damaged in transit:** must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery. Ship a replacement immediately and open a carrier damage claim. Reports after 48 hours go to Returns Desk L2.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, opened software and consumables (for example coffee filters and cleaning tablets).

## Goodwill and escalation

| Situation | Route | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Rewards Gold, up to 7 days past the window | Returns Desk L2 (goodwill decision) | 24 hours |
| Transit damage reported after 48 hours | Returns Desk L2 | 24 hours |
| Customer disputes a fee or decision | Returns Desk L2 | 24 hours |
| Safety concern (smoke, heat, sparks) | Product Safety desk, same day | 4 hours |
| Other customers past the window | Not eligible; offer trade-in or CarePlus | n/a |

## How to return

- At **any Larkspur store**, with the order number or receipt.
- By mail with a **prepaid return label** for orders over $50.
- **White-glove items** (large TVs) are collected at home by Halvard Final Mile; book the pickup slot with the customer.

## Refunds and price adjustments

- Refund to the original payment method in **3-5 business days**, or instant store credit.
- **Price adjustment:** if the price drops within **14 days** of purchase, refund the difference on request.

## Larkspur CarePlus protection plans

| Product price | 2-year plan | 3-year plan |
|---|---|---|
| up to $99.99 | $12 | $19 |
| $100 to $299.99 | $29 | $45 |
| $300 to $699.99 | $59 | $89 |
| $700 to $1,199.99 | $119 | $169 |
| $1,200 and above | $179 | $249 |

Accidental-damage add-on: **$60** for computing, **$25** for wearables. CarePlus covers repair or replacement after the manufacturer warranty ends and starts on the delivery date.

## Product advisories

**QA-26-031 — Vantor Brew 12-cup coffee maker (VAN-CM12).** Cracked housing near the screw boss has been reported on units built after 2026-10-26. Accept refund or exchange **without asking for proof of the cause** of the crack, at any point in the return window, and log the return with code **QA-26-031** so Quality can trace date codes. Do not suggest the customer caused the damage.

## Reply tone

- Start with the answer: what we will do and by when.
- Use the customer's name and the order number.
- Explain the rule in one sentence, not a policy quote.
- Offer the best option first (exchange for a defective TV, replacement for transit damage).
- Never promise something that needs L2 approval; say it has been requested and when the customer will hear back.
- State dates in full (for example "by 18 November 2026").
