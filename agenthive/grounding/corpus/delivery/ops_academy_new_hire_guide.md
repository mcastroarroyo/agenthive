# Wursta Academy — New-Hire Guide (your first 90 days)

**Owner:** People team with the Academy guild · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-07 · **Status:** current
Synthetic demo document. Chiron, the onboarding companion, answers from this guide and your onboarding record.

## Week 1 — get set up
- **Day 1:** Google Workspace account and the Academy LMS are ready (Service Desk SLA: 4 hours). Meet your manager and
  your onboarding buddy.
- **By day 5:** complete Security Awareness training (control WC-HR-01) and the acceptable-use acknowledgement.
- Request the tools your role needs (section 3). Your buddy helps you find the right projects and spaces.

## Benefits enrollment — 30-day window
You must enroll in health and other benefits **within 30 days of your start date**. If you miss the window you can
only enroll at the next open-enrollment period or after a qualifying life event. Chiron reminds you when 14, 7 and 2
days remain. Questions go to the People team, not to your manager.

## Your 30/60/90 plan by role
**Cloud Engineer** — Day 30: shadow a delivery squad, finish Terraform and landing-zone labs, first pull request
merged. Day 60: Associate Cloud Engineer certified; own one workstream task. Day 90: deliver a client-facing component
with review; demo a lab at the Academy guild.

**Data Engineer** — Day 30: BigQuery and Dataflow labs; pair on a client pipeline. Day 60: Associate Cloud Engineer
certified; own a data-quality check suite. Day 90: Professional Data Engineer certified (or exam booked); a pipeline in
production with review.

**Workspace Consultant** — Day 30: Workspace admin lab and migration methodology course; shadow one cutover weekend.
Day 60: Google Workspace Administrator certified; run a discovery workshop with a lead. Day 90: lead one migration wave
with supervision.

**Project Manager** — Day 30: Wursta Delivery Method (internal) completed; shadow two steering committees; learn SOW and
change-request process. Day 60: Cloud Digital Leader certified; co-run a project. Day 90: own a small project end to
end, including status reports and the risk log.

| Project Manager | Cloud Digital Leader | day 45 | 15 h |

- **Vouchers:** Wursta pays the exam fee for your first attempt. A retake is also paid with your manager's approval;
  respect the exam provider's waiting periods between attempts.
- **Study time:** up to 4 hours a week of working time for certification study during your first 90 days.
- **Book early:** book the exam at least 3 weeks before your target date; Chiron flags a certification as "at risk"
  when fewer than 21 days remain and no exam is booked.
- Labs run in your personal sandbox project — never in client environments.

## Check-ins
Your manager runs check-ins at day 30, 60 and 90 using this plan. Your buddy meets you weekly for the first month.
If you feel blocked (access, projects, workload), raise it in the next check-in or ask Chiron who to contact.
