## 1. Estimation model (EST-MODEL 3.2)
Effort is estimated per deliverable type with a base effort plus effort per unit, multiplied by complexity
(low 0.85, medium 1.0, high 1.3). Project management (12%) and engagement direction (3%) are added on top of
delivery hours. Estimates are delivery effort before contingency; risk is priced in the commercial model.

| discovery_assessment | engagement | Solutions architect 32 h, engagement director 8 h, change lead 8 h |

Every "high" complexity must be justified in the SOW assumptions. Indicative duration assumes a three-person core
team delivering about 110 hours per week.

## 3. Commercial models
- **Fixed fee:** net price (list minus discount) plus a risk premium of 8% (low), 15% (medium) or 22% (high
  complexity), rounded to the nearest 100 USD. Preferred for migrations with a clear inventory.
- **Time and materials:** estimate at contracted rates with a not-to-exceed amount 10% above the estimate;
  client notified at 80% burn. Preferred for AI and data work with discovery-dependent scope.
- Mixed SOWs are allowed: fixed fee for migration, T&M with NTE for the AI agent.

## 4. Margin and discounts
- Target gross margin: **40%**. Floor: **32%**. Below the floor requires VP Services and Finance approval as a
  margin exception, regardless of the discount.
- Discount approvals: up to 5% — Delivery Lead; up to 10% — Practice Director; up to 15% — VP Services; above 15% —
  CEO, exception only.
- Do not discount trainer and QA roles separately; discounts apply to the whole SOW.

## 5. Change orders
- Priced at the SOW's contracted rates (list rate minus the SOW's discount).
- Fixed-price SOWs: change orders carry a 10% contingency. T&M SOWs: change orders increase the NTE.
- Approval: up to 10,000 USD — Delivery Lead; up to 50,000 USD — Practice Director; above — VP Services.
- Change orders are valid for 30 days; no work starts before the client signs.

## 6. Expenses and travel
Not included in fees. Billed at cost only when pre-approved in writing; on-site floorwalking days must be listed in
the SOW with their number of days.
