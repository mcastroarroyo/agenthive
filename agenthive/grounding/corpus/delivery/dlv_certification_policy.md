# Google Cloud Certification Policy

**Owner:** Partner Office (Wursta) · **Version:** 3.0 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-15 · **Status:** current
Synthetic demo document. Specialization targets below are illustrative internal targets — always check Partner
Advantage for the current official requirements.

## 1. Why certifications matter
Google Cloud partner specializations and the client trust behind them depend on keeping enough consultants
certified at all times — not only on the day of the audit. A lapse in the wrong month can put a specialization at
risk. The Cert Notifier agent tracks every certification and reminds people early.

## 2. Validity
- Professional certifications (Cloud Architect, Data Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Cloud Security Engineer,
  Cloud Network Engineer, Cloud Developer, Cloud DevOps Engineer, Google Workspace Administrator): 2 years.
- Associate Cloud Engineer: 3 years.
- Foundational (Cloud Digital Leader, Generative AI Leader): 3 years.
Renewal means passing the current exam (or the renewal exam where Google offers one) before the expiry date.

## 3. Internal specialization targets (illustrative)
| Specialization | Target |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure | 4 Professional Cloud Architects, 3 Associate Cloud Engineers, 1 Professional Cloud Network Engineer |
| Data Analytics | 3 Professional Data Engineers, 1 Professional Cloud Architect |
| Machine Learning & Generative AI | 2 Professional ML Engineers, 4 Generative AI Leaders, 1 Professional Data Engineer |
| Security | 2 Professional Cloud Security Engineers |
| Work Transformation (Workspace) | 3 Professional Google Workspace Administrators, 2 Generative AI Leaders |

A specialization is **at risk** when coverage at the end of the look-ahead window (default 90 days) would fall
below target, and has a **gap** when it is already below target. A booked renewal exam counts as coverage.

## 4. Renewal process
1. Renewal window opens 90 days before expiry. From that day the consultant is eligible for a voucher.
2. Reminders at 90, 60, 30, 14 and 7 days before expiry, by email or chat, with the manager in copy.
3. Under 14 days with no exam booked: weekly reminder to the consultant and their manager.
4. Expired certifications are reported to the practice director in the monthly partner review.
5. The consultant books the exam and records the date; the tracker then stops reminding.

## 5. Vouchers and study time
- Budget: 12 exam vouchers per quarter across the company.
- Priority order: (1) renewals that protect an at-risk specialization, (2) renewals inside 30 days, (3) first
  certifications on the practice roadmap, (4) other renewals.
- Up to 16 hours of study time per exam, booked as internal time; managers plan it into staffing.
- A voucher not used within 60 days returns to the pool.

## 6. New certifications
Every consultant holds at least one Google Cloud certification within six months of joining. Delivery roles target
the Professional exam that matches their role; nearshore consultants follow the same policy and budget.

## 7. Reporting
Monthly certification health: expired, expiring in 30/60/90 days, specialization coverage now and at window end,
vouchers used. Shared in the partner review and with practice directors.
