# Social Media Policy (company channels)

**Owner:** Marketing & Brand team · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-05 · **Status:** current
**Applies to:** the Wursta company page on LinkedIn and the company account on X, and to any agent (Stentor) that drafts
or schedules posts for them. Synthetic demo document.

## 1. Principles
- Post to help our audience — practical lessons, measured results, useful agent capabilities.
- Every post is reviewed by a human before it is published, including posts drafted with AI assistance.
- When in doubt, don't post: ask the marketing lead.

## 2. What we may promote
- Content marked **approved for external use**: approved case studies, published blog posts and the Hive agent
  capabilities listed for launch.
- Case studies that are not approved (for example in-progress pilots) must not be referenced, even anonymously.
- Clients may be **named** only when the case study is both a named reference and approved for external use. Otherwise
  use the anonymised descriptor ("a regional transit agency").
- Never post client quotes, logos or screenshots without written approval recorded in the case study.

## 3. Claims and tone
- No absolute or unverifiable claims. The following phrases are banned: "guarantee", "guaranteed", "#1", "number one",
  "best in the world", "world-class", "100%", "risk-free", "never fails".
- Use measured results with their context ("−42% median triage time in a 12-week pilot").
- First person plural ("we"), plain language, one idea per post, one clear call to action.
- **No competitor mentions** — not by name, not by thinly veiled description.
- No commentary on politics, pending legal matters or client incidents.

## 4. Format rules per channel
| | LinkedIn | X |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 600-1,300 characters recommended; hard limit 3,000 | up to 280 characters |
| Hashtags | at most 5 | at most 2 |
| Links | at most 1 (in the body) | at most 1 |
| Images | alt text required | alt text required |

Approved hashtags: #GoogleCloud, #GoogleWorkspace, #GenAI, #AIAgents, #BigQuery, #Gemini, #DataAnalytics,
#PublicSector, #Insurance, #Logistics. Others need marketing approval.

## 5. When we post
Posting windows (US Central time):
- **LinkedIn:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday at 08:30 or 12:00.
- **X:** weekdays at 12:15 or 16:00.
Keep at least 20 hours between two posts on the same channel. Campaign weeks (for example the Agent Hive launch) are
planned as a mini-series: teaser, capability spotlights, then a case study.

## 6. Scheduling workflow
1. Draft the post per channel (different copy for LinkedIn and X — never cross-post the same text).
2. Validate it with the scheduling tool in **dry-run** mode and fix every error; warnings need a reason to keep.
3. Schedule only after the requester confirms. The tool returns a post id per scheduled post.
4. Marketing reviews the queue every Monday.

## 7. Crisis and pause
If there is a security incident, a client escalation in the news or a major platform outage, the marketing lead pauses
all scheduled posts. Do not post about the incident on social channels; route questions to communications.

## 8. Employees' personal accounts
Employees may share company posts. They must not disclose client names, internal metrics or roadmap details, and they
should make clear that opinions are their own.
