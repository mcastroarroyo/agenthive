# Change Management and Communications Playbook (Workspace migrations)

**Owner:** Change & Enablement practice (Sablewood) · **Version:** 3.2 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-28 · **Status:** current
Synthetic demo document.

## 1. Principles
1. People adopt what their manager uses. Leaders move first and say so.
2. Every user gets the right message at the right moment — tied to their wave, in their language.
3. Champions close the gap between a training session and Monday morning.
4. Measure adoption, not attendance: weekly active use, legacy-client use, tickets and pulse score.

## 2. Stakeholder management
Map stakeholders by influence (1-5) and impact (1-5):
- **Manage closely** (influence and impact 4+): weekly touchpoint, early preview, named role in the comms.
- **Keep satisfied** (influence 4+): monthly briefing, no surprises.
- **Keep informed** (impact 4+): wave-specific comms, champions in their team.
- **Monitor**: standard comms.
Resisters with influence 3+ get a personal plan before their wave's T-7 email: listen to the concern, show a
working answer (pilot users from their team, a specific fix), and invite them to sponsor a message.

## 3. Communication sequence per wave (cutover on Saturday)
| Touchpoint | When | Content | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-14 Announcement | Friday two weeks before | Date, why, what stays the same | Email |
| T-7 Prepare & train | Friday one week before | Training invitations, what changes, clean-up tips | Email + calendar invite |
| T-3 Action required | Tuesday before | Mobile guide, large-mailbox and PST notes | Email |
| T-1 Tonight is the night | Friday | Stop using Outlook after 18:00; Monday sign-in; support | Email + SMS to mobile users |
| DAY1 Welcome | Monday after | First steps, floorwalkers, help chat space | Email in Gmail |
| T+3 Tips & pulse | Thursday after | Top 5 tips, 3-question pulse survey | Email + Chat |
| T+10 Wrap-up | Second Thursday after | Source read-only date, where old items are, champions | Email |

Segments get add-on paragraphs or their own version: Spanish speakers (full Spanish version), mobile users,
legal-hold custodians (message from Legal), delegates and assistants, PST owners (PSTs are not migrated
automatically unless contracted), oversized mailboxes (older mail may appear later), shared-mailbox members.
Emails stay under 150 words with one call to action. Merge fields are written in square brackets, for example
[first_name] and [cutover_date].

## 4. Manager cascade
Managers receive the T-7 content two days earlier with a three-bullet talk track for their team meeting. Executives
send the T-14 announcement for their own department when they are a "manage closely" stakeholder.

## 5. Champions programme
- Target: at least 1 champion per 50 users in every department, 2 for frontline sites per shift.
- Champions complete M-CH-210 and are trained before their wave.
- On DAY1 champions floorwalk with the Sablewood team; for two weeks they hold a 15-minute daily drop-in.
- Recognition: monthly shout-out in the leadership update and a champion badge in Chat.
- Champions log every issue in the help chat space; they never change permissions themselves.

## 6. Adoption tactics (thresholds)
| Signal (30 days after cutover) | Threshold | Tactic |
|---|---|---|
| Drive weekly active users | below 60% | Shared-drive "move day" with champions; managers share files only via Drive links |
| Legacy client / old share still used | above 20% | Announce the read-only date; redirect old share paths; desk visits |
| Training completion | below 70% | Recorded sessions plus 15-minute catch-ups at shift change |
| Champion coverage | below 1 per 50 users | Recruit from power users; manager nomination |
| Tickets per 100 users | above 15 | Top-5 FAQ email, floorwalker extension |
| Pulse score | below 3.5 of 5 | Listening session with the department lead |

## 7. Frontline and multilingual sites
Shift huddles of 30 minutes at shift change, Spanish-first material, shared kiosks with sign-in cards, and champions
on every shift. Plant managers send the T-14 message themselves.

## 8. Measuring success
Weekly adoption dashboard per department: Gmail, Drive and Meet weekly active use, legacy-client use, training
completion, tickets per 100 users, champions per 50 users, pulse score. Review it in the steering committee.
