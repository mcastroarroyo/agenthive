# Client Enablement Curriculum (Workspace and Gemini)

**Owner:** Change & Enablement practice (Sablewood) · **Version:** 2026.3 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-05
Synthetic demo document.

## 1. Principles
- Train people in the seven days before their own cutover — earlier is forgotten, later is too late.
- Role-based paths: nobody sits through content they will not use.
- Live sessions for habits that change on day one (mail, calendar, files); self-paced for the rest.
- Every live module ends with a short knowledge check; pass mark 80%.
- Spanish material is mandatory wherever more than 10% of a group prefers Spanish (plants, warehouses, field sales).

## 3. Learning paths by role
- Executive: M-EX-050, M-GEM-110.
- Executive assistant: M-EU-101, M-EU-102, M-EA-150, M-GEM-110 (white-glove wave: assistants train first).
- End user: M-EU-101, M-EU-102, M-EU-103, M-GEM-110.
- Frontline (plants, warehouses, shared devices): M-FL-090 in a shift huddle, M-EU-103.
- Power user: M-EU-101, M-EU-102, M-PU-201, M-GEM-110.
- Champion: M-EU-101, M-EU-102, M-CH-210, M-GEM-110 — champions train before everyone else in their wave.
- Admin: M-ADM-301, M-ADM-302 — before the pilot wave.
- Legal / records: M-EU-101, M-EU-102, M-ADM-302, M-LEG-160.

Scheduling priority: admins, legal/records, champions, power users, assistants, executives, end users, frontline.

## 4. Planning rules
- Trainers deliver at most four live sessions a day; preparation adds 50% to delivery time.
- Cohort size never exceeds the module maximum; split by language before splitting by department.
- Frontline sessions run at shift change (06:45, 14:45, 22:45) on site; never longer than 30 minutes.
- Respect client freezes (for example Harborline: no training between 2026-11-15 and 2027-01-08).
- Gemini module only for licensed seats (pilot users first).

## 5. Targets
- Training completion before cutover: 85% (minimum 70% — below that the wave's change lead escalates).
- Knowledge-check pass rate: 80% first attempt.
- Day-one "how do I" tickets: under 8 per 100 users.

## 6. Quizzes
Questions come from the approved question bank per module (5-6 questions each, four options, one correct answer
with an explanation). Trainers get the answer key; learners do not. Questions are reviewed every release cycle so
they match current Workspace behaviour.

## 7. Materials
Slides, a one-page quick reference per module (English and Spanish), recorded sessions for absentees, and a
"what changes on Monday" card for frontline staff. Materials carry the client's branding; Sablewood branding only on
the cover.
