# Harborview Regional Transit Authority · RFP HRTA-2026-114 · Cloud Collaboration, Data Platform and Rider Assistant

Issued: 2026-10-26 · Questions due: 2026-11-16 · Proposals due: 2026-12-04, 17:00 local time
Issuer: Harborview Regional Transit Authority (HRTA) — fictional public transit agency, 2,400 staff, 6 bus depots,
1 light-rail line, ~210,000 weekday riders. Contract term: 3 years + 2 optional years. Budget ceiling: USD 1.2M
(implementation, years 1-3, excluding licences).

## 1. Background
HRTA runs an on-premises email and file-share environment that reaches end of support in 2027. Ridership, fare and
vehicle-location data sit in separate systems, and the customer-service centre answers ~3,000 rider questions a day by
phone and email. HRTA wants one modern collaboration platform, a governed data platform, and an AI rider assistant.

## 2. Scope
- Lot A: Migration of email, calendars and files to a cloud collaboration suite.
- Lot B: Cloud data platform for ridership, fares, on-time performance and maintenance data.
- Lot C: AI rider assistant for web and messaging channels (trip planning questions, service alerts, lost and found).
Bidders may bid one, two or all lots. Integrated bids for all lots are preferred.

## 3. Requirements
Mandatory requirements are marked (M); desirable requirements are marked (D).

- **R-01 (M)** — Migrate 2,400 mailboxes, calendars and 18 TB of file shares to Google Workspace with no more than 4 hours of email downtime per user.
- **R-02 (M)** — Provide a migration wave plan, a user communication plan and floor-walker support for each depot during cutover.
- **R-03 (M)** — Configure single sign-on with the authority's existing identity provider and enforce multi-factor authentication for all staff.
- **R-04 (M)** — Deliver a cloud data platform on BigQuery that ingests ridership, fare, automatic-vehicle-location and maintenance data with daily refresh.
- **R-05 (M)** — Provide role-based access controls and data classification so that rider personal data is restricted to authorised staff.
- **R-06 (M)** — Deliver on-time performance dashboards for operations managers, refreshed at least every 15 minutes.
- **R-07 (M)** — Deliver an AI rider assistant on the website and on a messaging channel answering service-alert, fare and lost-and-found questions in English and Spanish.
- **R-08 (M)** — The rider assistant must hand off to a human agent and must not answer questions outside approved transit information.
- **R-09 (M)** — The bidder must present at least two references for comparable public-sector or transit engagements completed in the last 3 years.
- **R-10 (M)** — Accessibility: all rider-facing interfaces must conform to WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **R-11 (M)** — All data must be stored and processed in the United States.
- **R-12 (D)** — The bidder holds an ISO/IEC 27001 certificate issued to the bidder's own organisation.
- **R-13 (D)** — Provide 24x7 production support for the rider assistant with a 1-hour response time for severity-1 incidents.
- **R-14 (D)** — Provide on-site support within 2 hours at all six depots for the first 90 days after go-live.
- **R-15 (D)** — Provide a knowledge-transfer programme and certification path for 10 authority IT staff.
- **R-16 (D)** — Propose a predictive maintenance pilot for the bus fleet using the data platform.

## 4. Evaluation criteria
- Technical approach and methodology: 40 points
- Relevant experience and references: 25 points
- Price: 25 points
- Local presence and support model: 10 points
A bidder that fails any mandatory requirement is disqualified. Desirable requirements are scored within the
technical approach.

## 5. Commercial terms (summary)
- Fixed price per lot with payment on milestones; 10% retention released at final acceptance.
- Liquidated damages of 0.5% of the lot value per week of delay on the go-live milestone, capped at 10%.
- The authority may terminate for convenience with 30 days' notice.

## 6. Submission
Proposals must include: executive summary (2 pages max), compliance matrix referencing every requirement, technical
approach per lot, project plan, team CVs, references, price schedule and exceptions to terms.
