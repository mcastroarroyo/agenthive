# Job brief — SOP: sales-to-implementation handoff

Submitted to Agent Workforce by Ines Duarte (Senior Implementation Consultant, Kitewing Software) on 2026-11-06. Synthetic demo document — Kitewing Software is fictional.

**Deliverable:** a standard operating procedure (SOP) as a Word document.
**Audience:** internal — Sales, Customer Success and Implementation teams.
**Priority:** standard.

## Why we need it

Three of the last five implementations started with missing information: the technician count was wrong, nobody knew about a data migration, and one customer's accounting package was not on the connector list. Implementation lost about a week each time. We want the handoff from a signed deal to a kicked-off implementation to be the same every time.

## Current process (rough notes from the team)

- When a deal is marked Closed Won in the CRM, the AE (Jamie Cho, Priya Natarajan or Ethan Caldwell) is supposed to fill in the handoff form — scope, plan, add-ons, technician count, legacy systems to migrate, accounting package, go-live target, customer sponsor and contacts. Often it's done late or half-empty.
- The Sales Engineer (Rosa Delgado) knows the technical details (integrations, data quality, security questionnaire answers) but is not always asked.
- Hannah Brooks (Head of CS) assigns an implementation consultant (Ines Duarte or Tomás Rivera) and a CSM, usually within two days.
- There should be an internal handoff call (AE, SE, implementation consultant, CSM) before the customer kickoff. It sometimes gets skipped.
- Customer kickoff should happen within 10 business days of signature.
- Finance (Maya Singh) needs the signed order form to invoice; implementation fees are invoiced at kickoff.

## Rules to respect

- One accountable owner for every step (company value: "small company, high ownership").
- Data migration needs at least two dry runs before cutover; no go-live between 21 December and 1 January.
- A blocker older than 5 business days goes to the project sponsor.
- Any scope change that adds more than 2 days of effort or $1,000 of cost needs a change request.

## KPIs we want to track

- Handoff form complete within 1 business day of Closed Won.
- Kickoff within 10 business days of signature.
- Number of implementations with scope surprises found after kickoff (target: zero).
