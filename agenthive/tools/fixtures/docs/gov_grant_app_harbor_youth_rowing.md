# Grant application GA-2026-0147 — Harbor Youth Rowing Collective (Youth Opportunity Fund, YOF-27)

Fictional application for demonstration purposes.

**Applicant:** Harbor Youth Rowing Collective (registered nonprofit since 2020) · 18 Cannery Wharf, Harborview (D1)
**Contact:** Executive Director Odile Fairweather · **Request:** $62,000 · **Project period:** March 2027 – February 2028
**Annual operating budget:** $410,000 · **Staff:** 5 full-time, 14 volunteer coaches

## 1. Need and target population

Youth unemployment in Harborview and Old Town is the highest in the city: 24% of residents aged 16–24 were neither in
school nor working in the 2025 city labour survey, against 13% citywide. Our current members reflect that need:
**72% of the 2026 cohort come from households enrolled in Alder Food Assist**, and 40% are the first in their family to
look for formal work. The programme will serve **48 young people aged 15–22** who live in Port Alder, recruited through
the two Harborview high schools and the DHS youth office.

## 2. Programme design — "Boathouse to Work"

Participants complete **paid 8-week apprenticeships** (20 hours a week at $11.50/hour) in three tracks run with the
Port Alder Marine Trades Association: boat maintenance and fibreglass repair, waterfront safety (leading to the
national lifeguard certificate), and boathouse operations (customer service and bookings). Each apprentice has a
mentor from a partner business and one evening of job-readiness training per week (CVs, interviews, financial
literacy). Two cohorts of 24 run in spring and summer 2027. Transport passes are provided for participants living more
than 2 km from the wharf.

## 3. Outcomes and evaluation

Targets: **80% completion**; **60% earn an industry certificate** (lifeguard or marine maintenance level 1); **50%
placed in a job, apprenticeship or further training within 3 months**. We will track attendance weekly and survey
participants at the end of the programme. We do not yet have a comparison group; this year we will start recording
placement status through partner employers. In 2025–26 our pilot (16 participants) reached 81% completion and 9
certificates.

## 4. Organisational capacity

Six years of operation; we completed a **2024 Youth Opportunity Fund grant of $40,000 on time** with all quarterly
reports accepted. Our **FY2025 financial statements were audited with no findings**. Programme manager Rui Tanaka has
eight years of workforce-programme experience. Board of nine includes a certified accountant and two marine-industry
employers. Letters of support from the Port Alder Marine Trades Association, Harborview High School and the DHS youth
office are attached.
