## What went well
- Ava's 14 new knowledge articles cut "how do I" tickets on maintenance agreements — Jonah thinks by about a third.

## What didn't go well
- September SLA breaches: two people on leave at the same time and nobody re-planned the rota.
- Our macros still describe the old Route Optimizer screens; we look outdated in replies.
- Bugs we report sit in engineering's backlog without an owner; customers ask us for dates we don't have. Zara: "I've chased the offline-photo bug for three weeks."
- Evening P1s: nobody is formally on call; whoever sees it first handles it.
- New hires (Grace, Samir) will need support shadowing — not planned yet.

## Ideas discussed
- Evening on-call rota for P1s (one person per week, with a stipend).
- Weekly 30-minute bug triage with engineering where every customer-impacting bug gets an owner and a target date.
- Update all macros after every release (release checklist item).
- Assist answer-quality tag in the support desk so product can see where Assist is wrong.
- Leave calendar reviewed in the Monday team meeting.
