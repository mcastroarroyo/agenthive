# Merchandising memo: Q4 overstock — campaigns before markdowns (fictional)

**From:** Head of Merchandising, Larkspur Home & Tech
**To:** CRM & Campaigns, Category Managers
**Date:** 2026-11-05

## Situation
Store inventory is carrying too much end-of-life and slow stock going into the peak weeks. The 2027 line-up
arrives in January (Lumetra Q9 TVs on 15 January) and we do not want to fund another round of 35-50% clearance
markdowns like last year.

## What I need by Monday
Ready-to-launch, targeted campaigns that start on **9 November** and finish **before the Black Friday quiet period
(23 November)** for TV & Home Theater, and inside November for the other categories.

Priority areas:
1. **Audio** — Aurion Buds 3 (end of life since Buds 4 launched) and the Aurion Party Speaker 2 left over from summer.
2. **Wearables** — Tessel Fit 5 bands (Fit 6 arrives in February).
3. **TV & Home Theater** — Lumetra Q8 55" sets and the Aurion SB700 soundbar.
4. **Kitchen** — Vantor AirCrisp air fryers (we over-bought for a promotion that was cancelled).

## Guardrails
- Stay within the category maximum discounts and the 18% margin floor (end-of-life exceptions need my sign-off).
- Respect contact caps — Deal Seekers are already at their limit this week.
- No straight discounts above 10% to Rewards Gold; use bonus points or bundles.
- Show me the margin versus doing nothing and clearing in January.

## Output
One brief per campaign with audience, offer, window, expected units and margin impact, plus copy for email, push
and social that the brand team can approve in one pass.
