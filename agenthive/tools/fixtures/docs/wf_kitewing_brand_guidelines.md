## Voice and tone

We sound like a great dispatcher: calm, clear and specific. Practical, warm and plain-spoken. We lead with the customer's outcome, not with our features, and we write for people who run vans, crews and service desks — not for software buyers.

Tone words: calm, clear, specific, practical, warm, plain-spoken

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Specific ("invoices out in 2 days instead of 9") | Vague ("faster invoicing") |
| Warm and human | Cute or jokey about customers' problems |
| Confident | Boastful or hyped |
| Helpful (a tip the reader can use today) | Salesy in every paragraph |

## Writing rules

1. Lead with the customer's outcome, then explain how we get there.
2. Be specific: numbers, dates and approved customer names beat adjectives.
3. Use plain verbs: help, cut, save, fix, finish, plan, schedule.
4. Keep the average sentence under 20 words and paragraphs to 4 sentences or fewer.
5. Write headings in sentence case.
6. Use US English spelling in customer-facing documents (optimize, organization, color).
7. At most one exclamation mark per piece; no emojis in documents (social posts: at most 2).
8. Write product names exactly as listed below.
9. Follow the claims policy for every number about results.
10. Do not compare ourselves with competitors by name in public content.

## Words we avoid

Never use: revolutionary, revolutionize, game-changer, synergy, disrupt, disruptive, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, leverage, unlock, seamless, seamlessly, next-gen, supercharge, 10x, robust, rockstar, ninja

## Logo usage

- The logo is the kite mark followed by the KITEWING wordmark. Where the artwork is not available, set the wordmark KITEWING in Montserrat Bold, letter-spaced, in Dispatch Navy.
- Clear space: at least the height of the "K" on every side.
- Minimum width: 24 mm in print, 120 px on screen.
- Use the navy logo on white and the white logo on Dispatch Navy or photos.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, outline or add shadows to the logo, and never place it on Kite Orange.
- Documents: logo top left on the title page and small in the header of content pages. Slides: wordmark on the title slide, small in the footer of content slides.

## Document layout

- Title page: title in Dispatch Navy, a Kite Orange rule beneath it, subtitle in Ink, then "Prepared for" and the date.
- Footer: "Kitewing Software · kitewing.example" and the page number. Add "Confidential" for proposals and internal documents.
- Tables: Dispatch Navy header row with white text, Cloud Grey banding.
- Slides: 16:9; title slide on Dispatch Navy with white text; content slides on white with a Kite Orange accent bar; at most 6 bullets per slide.
- Spreadsheets: Dispatch Navy header row with white text, frozen header row, filters on.
