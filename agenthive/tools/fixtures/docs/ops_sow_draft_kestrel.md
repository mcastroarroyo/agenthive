# Statement of Work SOW-KMG-03 (DRAFT v0.3) · Kestrel Media Group · Looker Semantic Layer & GenAI Content Tagging

Parties: Wursta ("Wursta") and Kestrel Media Group ("Client") — fictional regional media group (radio, print, digital).
Master agreement: MSA-KMG-2025. Draft prepared by: sales team, 2026-11-02. Status: not yet reviewed by legal.

## 1. Scope of services
1.1 Wursta will design and build a Looker semantic layer over the Client's BigQuery data warehouse covering
advertising revenue, subscriptions and audience data, including but not limited to all data sources the Client may
identify during the project.
1.2 Wursta will build a GenAI content-tagging service that tags articles and audio transcripts with topics, people and
places using Gemini on Vertex AI.
1.3 Wursta will provide ongoing support and enhancements as needed after go-live.
1.4 Deliverables include dashboards, data models, documentation, training, etc.

## 2. Client responsibilities
2.1 The Client will provide access to its systems when possible.
2.2 Wursta shall be responsible for obtaining data from the Client's third-party ad-server vendor.

## 3. Timeline
3.1 The project will start on 2026-11-16 and go live by 2027-01-29.
3.2 Any delay in go-live will incur a penalty of 2% of the total fees per day of delay.

## 4. Acceptance
4.1 Each deliverable will be accepted when it is to the Client's sole satisfaction.
4.2 Wursta will provide unlimited revisions to dashboards until acceptance.

## 5. Performance commitments
5.1 Wursta guarantees that the content-tagging service will achieve at least 99% accuracy on all content types.
5.2 Wursta will respond to any support request within 1 hour, 24x7.

## 6. Fees and payment
6.1 Services are billed on a time-and-materials basis at the rates in Schedule A.
6.2 Invoices are payable net 90 days from receipt.
6.3 Travel expenses will be reimbursed at cost with prior written approval.

## 7. Intellectual property
7.1 All intellectual property created or used by Wursta in performing the services, including pre-existing materials,
tools and accelerators, will be assigned to the Client upon payment.

## 8. Liability
8.1 Wursta's liability under this SOW shall not be limited.

## 9. Change control
9.1 Changes to scope will be agreed informally by email between the project managers.

## 10. Termination
10.1 The Client may terminate this SOW for convenience at any time without payment for work in progress.

## 11. Assumptions
11.1 The Client's data is of sufficient quality for reporting.

## 12. Data protection
12.1 Wursta will use commercially reasonable efforts to protect Client data and will process it only in the United
States.
