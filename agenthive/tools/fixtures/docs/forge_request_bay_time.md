**Submitted through:** Agent Forge Home ("What type of agent do you need?") · **Client:** Summit Tire & Auto (prospect)
**Builder:** Jonah Pike · **Date:** 2026-11-06 · *Fictional demo request.*

## The request (as written by the client's service manager)

> We need an agent that gets data from every employee on their bay times. We run two shops (Mesa and Chandler),
> 14 technicians, 10 bays. Everything is in Tekmetric. Right now I export the time clock and the repair orders every
> Friday and fight with a spreadsheet for two hours. I want to see, per tech and per bay, how long they were clocked
> in versus how much labor they actually billed, and which bays sat empty. A daily sheet would be great and a summary
> for me on Monday mornings. Don't change anything in Tekmetric.

## Notes from the builder's call

- "Bay time" means time a technician spends working a repair order in a given bay (from job labor lines), compared
  with clocked time from the time clock.
- They close at 18:00; some techs finish jobs after close and clock out late. A few forget to clock out.
- The service manager reads everything on a phone. Owners want a weekly view per shop.
- Time zone: both shops are in Arizona.
- They already use Google Workspace (Gmail, Sheets).
- Not decided yet: who gets the Monday summary besides the service manager, and whether the summary should be sent
  or saved as a draft for review.
- Summit is a prospect: everything stays in the sandbox until they sign.
