# Case file CLM-2026-0083 — Okafor v. City of Port Alder (vehicle damage, 1388 Harbor Street)

Office of the City Attorney — Litigation & Claims. Fictional case file for demonstration purposes.
Assigned: Nadia Brennan, Deputy City Attorney · Adjuster: Pauline Wexford, Risk Management.

## 1. Claim form (filed 2026-07-28)

Claimant: Amaka Okafor, Eastgate. Incident: **11 May 2026, about 21:40**, Harbor Street eastbound near number 1388.
"My car hit a large pothole that filled the lane. Two tyres burst and the front rim cracked. The street was wet and
there was no warning sign or cone." Amount claimed: **$4,470** — Eastgate Tire & Auto invoice 5521 for $3,870 (two
tyres, one rim, alignment) and $600 for an 8-day rental car while parts were ordered.

## 2. 311 history (from the 311 system)

- **SR-2026-061744**, received 2 May 2026 08:30 by web: "Large pothole in the eastbound lane near 1388, approx 45 cm
  wide and 10 cm deep." Triaged **P2 (72-hour SLA)**. Harbor Street is a designated arterial under the 311 Service
  Standards.
- 4 May: crew inspection — "cold patch scheduled, WO-2026-22817; asphalt plant supply shortage". No completion entry.
- 8 May: duplicate SR-2026-064102 merged — "pothole getting bigger".
- 19 May 11:00: permanent hot-mix repair; request closed.

## 3. Adjuster notes (P. Wexford)

- 12 Aug: Spoke with Streets Superintendent Lena Ortiz. She believes a cold patch was placed on 4 or 5 May but cannot
  find a crew log; materials shortage lasted "about a week". No contemporaneous record of crew diversion.
- 20 Aug: Claimant provided **dashcam stills** with an overlay showing **34 mph** in the 30 mph zone, wet road, street
  lights on, pothole visible as a dark patch about one car length ahead. Original video file requested — not received.
- 2 Sep: Invoice 5521 verified with Eastgate Tire & Auto. Rental period: shop confirms parts arrived on day 6; 8 days
  of rental is two days longer than needed.
- 8 Sep: Claim rejected in writing (standard rejection letter, no reasons given).

## 4. Demand letter (21 Oct 2026)

Brightwater Law Group (Simone Achebe) demands **$5,670**: $4,470 damages plus $1,200 attorney fees, and states the
client will file a limited civil action in Alder County Superior Court if not paid within 30 days. The letter cites the
311 report of 2 May as proof that the City "knew about the hazard for nine days".

## 5. Internal email (15 Sep 2026, L. Ortiz to G. Achterberg)

"Our work order shows a cold patch was scheduled on 4 May but there is no completion entry. Coastline Paving was not
working on that block in May."

## 6. Open points for the review

1. Was the cold patch actually placed? If so, did it fail before 11 May?
2. Effect of the P2 triage on an arterial street (standard requires P1, 24 hours).
3. Comparative fault from the dashcam speed on a wet road.
4. Reasonable rental period and whether attorney fees are recoverable.
5. Records to preserve: 311 records, WO-2026-22817, crew and materials logs for 2–19 May, dashcam original, photos.
6. Related public-records request PRR-2026-0418 (media) touches the same email thread — coordinate before release.
