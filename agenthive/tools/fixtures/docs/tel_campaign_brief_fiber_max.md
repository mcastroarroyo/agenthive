# Campaign brief: Kestrava Fiber Max 2 Gig holiday upgrade (fictional)

**Brand:** Kestrava Telecom · **Owner:** Consumer Marketing · **Window:** 16 November - 31 December 2026

## Objective
Move existing Fiber Max 1 Gig households to 2 Gig before the holidays, and win new fiber customers in the
Port Calder and Ashford Vale footprint. Target: 6,500 upgrades and 1,800 new connections.

## Offer
- Kestrava Fiber Max 2 Gig for **$65/mo for 12 months**, then $85/mo.
- Free Kestrava Wi-Fi 7 Gateway (worth $180) and no installation fee for orders by 31 December 2026.
- Existing 1 Gig customers keep their current contract end date.

## Audience
1. Existing Fiber Max 1 Gig customers with 3+ connected devices streaming in the evening.
2. Prospects at addresses passed by fiber who browsed the coverage checker in the last 60 days.

## Key message
Everyone at home can stream, game and work at the same time — without the wait.

## Proof points
- Symmetric 2 Gig speeds: uploads as fast as downloads.
- Wi-Fi 7 Gateway with whole-home coverage up to 3,000 sq ft.
- Rated fastest home internet in Port Calder* — Source: Fictional Speed Index, Q3 2026.

## Channels and deliverables
| Channel | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| Push (app) | 2 variants, title + body |
| Email | Subject, preheader, body (max 180 words), CTA |
| Social | 1 post for the brand page |
| SMS | 1 message to opted-in customers |

## Mandatory
- Price claims: "Taxes and fees extra. T&Cs apply." in email; "T&Cs apply." in push, SMS and social.
- SMS must end with "Reply STOP to opt out".
- Use the product names exactly: Kestrava Fiber Max, Kestrava Wi-Fi 7 Gateway.
- Offer end date must be visible in email: 31 December 2026.
